# Session 003 - We Meet Gearson

**Date:** 2025-09-01
**Players Present:** [[Wart]] [[Talara]] [[Misty]] [[Gearson]] [[Klix]] [[Abeiva]]
**Duration:** 3 hours

## Session Summary

We started the session with our heroes stuck in a dark passage way towards the back of the Great Hive after vanquishing [[Queen Sphesia]].  As they travel on they end up meeting up with Borth and having a long, well-deserved rest. In the night Talara, Misty, and Wart are confronted in their dreams with demons of their flaws. Jealousy for Misty, Doubt for Talara, and Hubris for Wart.

The next day they travel on getting to the [[Luminos Caverns]]. From the exit which was carved into the side of a cliff the spy a tough little #goblin [[Gearson]] fighting an [[Luminous Cavern Burrower]] alone. In a pretty quick battle our heroes were able to make quick work of the three burrowers but not with out a few mishaps. In one good strike one of the Burrowers was hit and sprayed its acid blood all over [[Misty]]. [[Abeiva]] was also able to get a big cinder grasp, but also took a devistating shot once again.

After the battle the group headed towards the city of [[Nishunos]] a dwarven settlement build in these massive caverns. Known for trade they enter the city in a bustling market. They do some shopping at an apothecary and try to send some letters at the dwarven post master to mixed success. [[Talara]] head to the chapel of [[Moridin]] where she found out that their curse would take more than a simple healer or priest could lift. She also spoke with her mother who was a refugree from the recently destroyed [[Lucita]]. Her mother asked her to return to Lucita to see what was left. She also said that her father [[Storn]] headed off to the [[Commonwealth]] to join with the [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]].

[[Abeiva]] and [[Klix]] went to meet the merchant baron Stonehand to sell their beads for a pretty penny. They were also offered a bargin for to chestfuls of gold to go and "handle" the local Fungril Tribe the [[Mok]]. They managed to hold their own and gave an answer which allows them to possibly back out, Stonehand gave them a calling rune if need be. Abeiva also realized that Stonehand is the owner of the [[Golden Goose High Fashion]].

After meeting back up the group decides to climb the long scaffoling path up to the peak of the mountian to meet with the goblin tinkers there. We met [[Fizbit]] an excitable guy who has been working on a long range gliding machine. [[Gearson]] asked about his father [Gearson Sr.] who has been getting up in years and been spleeping most of the days.

# Session 001 - Buzzing Jungle 1

**Date:** 2025-08-09
**Players Present:** 5
**Duration:** Overtime 5hrs (included a session 0)

## Session Summary
We met our players on the road. Seeing smoke on the horizon they headed towards the source finding [[Erris Othel]] along the road who informed the players that her wife [[Alma Blackgrove]] was taken. The players then trekked into the [[Buzzing Jungle]] choosing to take the [[Ora River Crossing]]. They were ambushed by a bear and a glass viper. [[Abeiva]] was grabbed in the jaws of the bear. They quickly handily defeat the enemies and continue on.

After taking a vote they chose to circle back onto the road and arrived at the [[The Yellowjacket's Sting Tavern]] where they met [[Kalor]] and [[Gorse]]. [[Klix]] also tried to meet [[Borth]] but her demeanor was less than helpful. Though they did learn that maybe the honey is not something to consume. [[Kalor]] and [[Gorse]] both let in on how the jungle is much more prosperous now. [[Abeiva]] purchased two lbs of [[Jungle Beads]] . They eventually choose to push on to the [[Jungle Priory]] before the evening.

The party continued on meeting some [[Akavarii Raiders]] on the road, but not choosing to fight them. As the rain begins our heros trudge up to the priory where they meet a kindly old headmaster [[Albric]], who they later found out was not as he seemed. [[Misty]] did eat a piece of fruit. [[Albric]] took his leave and let the heroes explore the priory.

[[Wart]] went to the chapel with [[Erris Othel]] where he prayed to [[Bartholomew]]. [[Misty]] and [[Abeiva]] went into the catacombs below the priory finding empty and intricate coffins. At the end of the dark hall the dispel a glamored wall revealing a shire to [[Kathral]]. [[Klix]] and [[Telara]] head outside where [[Klix]] talks to the stately sycomore tree who stands in the priory. The tree tells him that there is no garlic in the garden. [[Talara]] stand on the observatory deck and looks to the moon. In that moment [[Sehanine]] speaks to her letting her know something is deeply wrong here and that [[Sehanine]] is not welcomed. Eventually [[Abeiva]] feels a pit in their stomach realizing that this is a vampires lair.

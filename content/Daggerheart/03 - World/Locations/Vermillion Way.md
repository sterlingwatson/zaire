# Vermillion Way

## Basic Info
- **Region:** [[Lu Shin City]]
- **Type:** #neighborhood
- **Danger Level:** #safe
- **Population:** 3673

## Description
### Visual Description
- The Vermillion Way is a neighborhood of [[Lu Shin City]] where officers of the [[Imperial Army]] are given residences. In the tiered mountain city of Lu Shin the Vermillion Way is about half way up the city. 
- The titular Vermillion Way is a main street that runs NE to SW with a view of [[Lu Shin Bay]]. It is adored with flowing red fabrics, red paper lanterns, and businesses painted red and gold. The railing on the street are ornately carved with lion statues every 50 feet or so which also over look the bay.
- The streets are paved with neat cobblestones. 
- ![[Pasted image 20250825170122.png]]
- Further in the neighborhood the reliance on red diminished as the streets curve up the hills with many apartments tucked into the hills. The buildings are tidy, nice, and not run down.
- Homes are given to officers and their families.
- A parallel street, smaller and further up the hill called Pale Street serves as the market street for the local families. Specializing in spicy dry noodles, fresh fish, and vegetables it is a great place to eat. There is a small park call Frog's Crag which often serves as a place for kids to play and have a picnic.

### Atmosphere
- On the Vermillion Way itself things can get rowdy in the evening as officers are seen partying enjoying and enjoying themselves. 
- Folks from the higher levels and neighborhood sometimes come down to enjoy themselves in the Vermillion Way. Though the inverse is not true, people of lower classes are not legally allowed to go up to the higher tiers.
- In the back residential streets, things are quiet and peaceful. Lots of leafy green trees, with fresh water streams that come from higher in the city.

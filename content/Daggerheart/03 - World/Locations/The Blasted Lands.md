# The Blasted Lands

## Overview

A region that specifically denotes the surface of an eastern area of [[Solira]] which is completely uninhabitable as it is deeply scared with magical damage and constant storm. [[Sar'eth]] is rumored to be deep in the region recovering. Under the earth we can find the [[Dwarven Triple Alliance]] in cities carved safely underground.

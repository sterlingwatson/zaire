# Dwarf

## Overview

Dwarves were the first mortals, created by [[Or]] — or more accurately, by [[Kavra]] and [[Orunn]] — from mud and rock. They are of the land itself, and the orbital cycles of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] do not touch them. This is a point of quiet pride. The world wobbles. The dwarves do not.

## Culture in Zaire

Dwarven culture is the oldest continuous civilization on [[Solira]] (which they call **Tharrok**). They keep the [[Dating Systems|Dwarven Deep Count]], worship [[Kavra]] and [[Orunn]] rather than the simplified [[Or]] of the surface pantheon, and venerate ancestor spirits called the Reckoned. The [[The Blasted Lands|Dwarven Triple Alliance]] is the primary dwarven political entity in the current era. See [[The Dwarven Faith]] for their religious tradition.

# Giant

## Overview

Giants were created by [[Ennem]], god of vitality, to use their large bodies to shape the earth and rear livestock. They are draconic-rooted, with their power resonating with [[Elysium]].

## Culture in Zaire

Giant communities are diverse — some are pastoral herders living in the tradition Ennem intended, others have become mercenaries or laborers in the free kingdoms. [[Borth]] of the Rockbiter Clan represents the martial tradition among giants.

# The Legendary Heroes

> *Six people who should never have been in the same room, who ended an empire. History has made them into symbols. They were messier than that.*

---

## Overview

The Legendary Heroes were the core of [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]]'s coalition during the **Campaign of Broken Chains** (3131–3137). Six individuals whose backgrounds had almost nothing in common, united by circumstance and the specific pressure of living under the Elven Empire's final years. They are the subject of the bonus sessions.

The revolt they led and the deal Bartholomew struck with [[Sar'eth]] at its end shaped every political and spiritual reality that the main campaign now navigates.

See also: [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker]], [[Sar'eth]], [[The Hooded One]], [[Timeline]]

## The Six

### [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]]
The leader. An extraordinary figure who received great magical power directly from [[Yaaw]] in a ritual overnight in 3131, in a village far to the north. She united peoples who had no history of cooperation. At the revolt's end she made the deal with [[Sar'eth]] that no one else knew about, and sealed herself away in the volcano alongside him rather than allow her own power to shape the world that came after.

History has erased that she was a woman. The [[Cult of the Radiant Sun|Cult of the Radiant Sun]] has made her into whatever they need her to be.

### [[Kal Ral Ed]]
A Clank — built by the drakona for war, and struggling with that. The drakona created [[Kal Ral Ed|Ed]] as a weapon prior to the period when [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt was dismantling their civilization. He spent many years practicing gardening and pacifism, slowly replacing parts of himself from tools of war to tool of creation.  By the time Ed found Bartholomew's coalition, the civilization that had built them was nearly gone, and he reasoned there needed to be an end to the elves and slavery if there was to be peace in the end.

They are also a living artifact of drakona craft, which means they may carry knowledge, or even encoded memory, of what drakona civilization was before the Hunt.

### [[Mazz]]
An [[The Aethris|Aethris]] at the time of the Legendary Heroes campaign, he is a person shaped by the Aethris' persecution under the Elven Empire, carrying the full weight of what it meant to be hunted for guiding the dead with dignity.

He is already in contact with a [[The Thornwood Court|Thornwood Court]] patron of Madness. Whether that relationship is visibly problematic yet, or whether it looks like a straightforward warlock pact, is a question for the bonus sessions.

### [[Torris]]
An [[The Aethris|Aethris]] cleric who takes the moving of souls with more seriousness than anyone else in the group, including Mazz. Where Mazz carries the political wound of Aethris persecution, Torris carries the **functional obligation**, he has seen what happens to souls that aren't guided and he cannot unknow it.

She and Mazz are both Aethris, both agents of [[Corvina]], but their relationship to that work is different in ways that may put them in tension during the campaign. Mazz is angry. Torris is dutiful. Both responses to the same persecution are real.

## [[The Coalition's Legacy]]

The Legendary Heroes did not build institutions. They ended one. The world that came after was shaped not by their design but by their, particularly [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]]'s absence, by the space they left when the revolt succeeded.

This is partly why the [[Cult of the Radiant Sun|Cult of the Radiant Sun]] has been able to claim Bartholomew's name so effectively. There was no institution, no established lineage of followers, no written account Bartholomew left. Just the legend, and legends can be occupied.

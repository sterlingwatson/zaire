# Redcaps

> *They must keep killing to survive. If the cap dries, they die. There is a poetry to this that none of them are in a position to appreciate.*

---

## Overview

Redcaps are what most of the [[Gilded Court]] gnomes became after [[The Hooded One]] turned his attention to them. They are the corrupted remnant of a court that once prided itself on civilization and cleverness — now reduced to creatures that must continuously shed blood just to exist.

See also: [[The Gilded Court]], [[On Gnomes]], [[The Hooded One]]

---

## Nature

In folklore, a Redcap is a creature that soaks its cap in the blood of its victims. If the cap dries out, the creature dies. This is not metaphor for the Gilded Court gnomes — it is **literal karmic corruption.** A court that dealt in the lives and blood of others now must continuously spill blood merely to survive. They are trapped in the violence they once facilitated, unable to stop, unable to rest.

## The Hooded One's Use of Them

[[The Hooded One]] destroyed the Gilded Court **first** when he took over the [[Fey Wild]]. He views betrayal from within as the unforgivable sin. What he left behind — the Redcaps — he now uses as shock troops. They are already broken. They are already hungry. They serve his purposes without needing to be persuaded.

This is its own kind of cruelty: he turned the collaborators into weapons, removing even the possibility of their reckoning with what they did.

---

## In the Fey Wild

Redcaps are found throughout the corrupted portions of the [[Fey Wild]], particularly in the ruins of what were once Gilded Court territories. They are dangerous and largely mindless in their hunger, though there may be traces of what they were underneath.

## The Uncorrupted Few

Not all gnomes became Redcaps. A small number hid rather than doubling down when the corruption came. They are not Redcaps, but they live with the knowledge that their kin are. See [[On Gnomes]].

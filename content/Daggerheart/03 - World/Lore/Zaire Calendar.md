# The Zaire Divine Calendar
*A 13-Month Year of 364 Days*

The calendar structure, 13 months of 28 days, each dedicated to one of the gods, is shared across [[Solira]]. Only the year numbering differs between civilizations. See [[Dating Systems]] for the four calendars in use: **Free Reckoning (FR)**, **Imperial Reckoning (IR)**, **Jade Empire Dynastic (JE)**, and **Dwarven Deep Count (DC)**.

---

## Calendar Structure
**364 days total** • **13 months** • **28 days per month** • **4 weeks per month**

---

## Calendar Notes

- Each month has exactly 4 weeks of 7 days
- Seasonal transitions align with divine domains
- Major festivals typically occur during the middle week of each month
- The year begins with Yaaw (mystery/death) and cycles through to Malort (art/love) before renewal
- The calendar structure is shared between the [[Dating Systems|Imperial Reckoning (IR) and Free Reckoning (FR)]] — only the year numbering differs
- Moon phase data above is given for **FR 812–813**, the current campaign years

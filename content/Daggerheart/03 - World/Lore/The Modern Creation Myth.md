[The One] set in motion all time, the Universe but put nothing in it. They created a garden from chaos and set order to it. And the universe existed like that for millennia. Once while The One was sleeping, from a dream, a moon rose out The One's head. Bright white and full of potential. Fascinated by a creation of unconscious creativity [The One] was mystified. 

[[Sehanine]] or the moon, was lonely here with a being that was so large that it could not understand the feeling of such a small thing as the moon. Sehanine coaxed [The One] to sleep again, and in that sleep manipulated their dreams to bear a partner. From [The One]'s heart a firey ball was created, full of energy and life the Sun [[Asel]] was brought into the heavens. 

For millennia more, it was just the three entities, [The One] eventually grew tied of [[Asel]] and [[Sehanine]]'s love as he felt as if he had been tricked. One day, from their liver [The One] produced a chunk of flesh and molded it into the [[Mortal Plane]], the planet [[Or]] and placed it in between the Sun and Moon. In their separation [[Sehanine]] wept creating the stars in the night sky.

[[Or]] a stout and gruff fellow felt that his realm was boring, a brown chunk of rock and mud created to satisfy an increasingly cruel god's desire to cause pain. They created the Dwarves from mud and rock to keep them company in the barren lands. 

[The One] was of two minds, in one they felt as if Or had created something that was not their right and in the other they were fascinated by the small folk working and building in the earth. They challenged the Sun and Moon to create their own creatures, Humans and Elves respectively. But these creatures were not as tough as dwarves and could not live below the surface. From their lung [The One] bore the seas and the wind and gave them to [[Comforce]], from their stomach they made the plants, giving [[Lorta]] life, and from their spleen they gave the creatures vitality, in turn giving birth to [[Ennem]]. They were each given an opportunity to create a mortal as well. Comforce made the Orcs, strong and fit for sailing. Lorta, the Firbog gentle and kind, to cultivate the land. Ennem, the Giants, to use their large bodies to shape the earth and rear livestock.

Things were good for a time. A handful of creature living a life of peace and harmony. Though the mortals could have no children as they were children themselves. The gods petitioned [The One] to their children, the same gift once given to them. From their ovaries [The One] gave the mortals the ability to have children and populate. [[Seedlip]] was born and brought halfings with him into the world, small in stature so they may always remind [The One] of children.

[The One] quickly realized though that there would not be enough space as there was not death for these children of the gods. Everyone was immortal, and from their intestines they introduced death and decay to the mortals, creating a cycle of life and death. [[Xail]] was born and something started to change. [The One] had started to get weaker. [[Sehanine]] asked the one to make a deal to save her children from this fate of mortality, she would take Xail as a sister and allow the elves the ability to have many fewer children, in return give some of her power back to [The One], who accepted.

In turn from the pain this curse of death from their eyes [The One] created [[Malort]] who gave them all the thrill of romantic love and the beauty of art. Then from their tongue [The One] made [[Rethel]] who gave them cuisine and the merriment of sharing a meal. Malort made the Simia to swing in the trees and Rethel made the Ribbets to swim in the blue seas.

This part has two tellings in our story depending on how the locals feel about the elves. In one telling at the meeting were [Sehanine] is to give power back to [The One] she tricks them. As [The One] reaches their hand out to receive Sehanine she draws a dagger and cuts off [The One]'s hands bears a twins from each hand [[Amaric]] and [[Storse]]. though born of violence and greed they give though vices to the mortals themselves, especially the elves. In these tellings it is for revenge on [The One] for separating [[Asel]] and herself. In more gracious tellings when [The One] is made to look like the bad one, [The One] asks for Sehanine to return the mind of the [The One] and to give of herself and the elves freely. She refuses and [The One] creates greed and war to punish the mortals.

Nevertheless in the end, led by the warlike Amaric, the gods lead a revolt and kill their parent, and the mortal's grandparent in cold blood. From their corpse, rotting in the wastes of eternity [[Yaaw]] is born. [[Yaaw]] with the last powers of creation as given to them by [The One] creates the three parallel realms to the [[Zaire]], the [[Fey Wild]], [[The Hushed Lands]], [[Elysium]].

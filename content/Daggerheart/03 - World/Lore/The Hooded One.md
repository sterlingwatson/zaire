# The Hooded One

> 

---

## Overview

The Hooded One is the primary antagonist of the current arc. He is not a single being but a **fusion**, a mortal warlock and his [[Thornwood Court]] fae patron, merged into something neither of them fully intended. He represents what happens when righteous vengeance is given infinite time and unlimited power.

---

## Origins

### The Warlock

The Hooded One began as an **[[Aethris]]**, a people associated with [[The Realms Below]] who were tasked with guiding the souls of the dead onward. The Aethris were heavily persecuted by the Elven Empire, because their work implied that the souls of "lesser beings" passed into a form of spiritual immortality — a concept the elves found threatening to their own sense of cosmic superiority.

He was one of the legendary heroes of the slave revolt that ended the Elven Empire. A player character in a prior age.

*Notes to expand: his name before the merging, his role in the revolt, what he lost to the elves personally.*

### The Patron

His patron was an ancient being of the [[Thornwood Court]], the oldest and most primal of the [[Four Diminished Courts]]. She was already augmenting herself through a pact with an entity tied to [[The Hushed Lands]] when she found the warlock. 

What he believed was a partnership was closer to a parasitic relationship. She fed on him while allowing him to believe he was in control.

*Notes to expand: her name, her history in the Thornwood Court before the corruption, what she wanted.*

### The Merger

At some point, the boundary between patron and warlock dissolved entirely. The result is the Hooded One, driven by the warlock's **hatred for the elves** and the patron's hatred for the [[Sehanine]]

In their fusion, both lost their individual restraint.

---

## Motivations

- Destruction of the remaining elven power structures, particularly [[Sehanine]] and [[Xail]]
- Vengeance on behalf of the [[Fey Wild]] and the Aethris.

---

## The Hooded One and the Fey Wild

The Hooded One has taken over much of the [[Fey Wild]]. Their hatred is not contained, it **infects**. The corruption spreads through the Fey like a sickness, warping the [[The Thornwood Court]] into feral shadows of themselves, and accelerating the transformation of [[The Gilded Court]] gnomes into [[Redcaps]].

He has also infected the [[Anchors]] that lock the Fey Wild near the [[Mortal Plane]]. This sets up a dangerous convergence, when the anchors are destroyed, it is hard to say what will happen.

---

## Role in the Current Arc

The Hooded One is positioned to **kill Sehanine** at the climax of this arc when the anchors are destroyed. Whether this is justice, tragedy, or both is deliberately unresolved.

His relationship to [[Sar'eth]] is worth considering both want the anchors down, but for very different reasons and with very different ends in mind.

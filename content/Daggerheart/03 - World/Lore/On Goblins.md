# On Goblins
---

## Overview

Goblins in the [[Mortal Plane]] are not the goblins of the [[Fey Wild]]. Generations of diaspora have made them something distinct, communities shaped by displacement, preservation, and ingenuity.

See also: [[On Faeries]], [[Hollow Court]]

---

## Origins

Goblins were created by [[Yaaw]] alongside the faeries when the divine corpse of [[The One]] gave rise to the [[Fey Wild]]. They are not children of gods the way the mortal races are.

The Elven Empire took goblins from the Fey Wild in vast numbers. They were the most taken, the least protected, and the most systematically targeted. The [[Hollow Court]], the Fey Court that used to be called The [Boundless Court], bears witness to the scale of what was lost.

---

## The Diaspora

Goblins in the mortal realm maintain their identity through:

- **Shared texts**: a core body of literature, history, and law that travels with every community regardless of where they settle
- **Shared practice**: festivals and rituals tied to the seasonal memory of the Fey Wild.
- **Tight community bonds**: goblin neighborhoods, regardless of city or nation, tend toward self-sufficiency and mutual support

---

## Skills and Reputation

Goblins are renowned as tinkerers, builders, and scholars. They construct their own models of Clanks and are among the most inventive engineers in the mortal realm. Their bookishness is cultural. 

They are generally welcomed in most communities around [[Zaire]] as friendly and helpful folk. They have a private distrust of elves often, yet do not share stories from their culture about their past with others, preferring to keep that information transmitted exclusively in Gobish. To that end it is taboo to teach another person Gobish.

Their Clanks follow in the legends of the Golem in which they are given live by writing in the old Goblin language "breath" or "life" or similar. The ancient Goblin Language as carried with them from the Fey still retains a power of creation. This is similar to the runic magic the [[Dwarves]] practice but has more raw power.

---

## Gobalania

Far to the east, goblins govern a full city-state, [[Gobalania]], which is the capital city of the [[Barony of Blanc]], one of the few places in the world that is unambiguously Gobish. Most goblins have heard about it, even those who will never travel there. It functions as a symbol as much as a place. That symbol being that even small folks can build big.

# The Chrysalis Court
---
## Overview

The Chrysalis Court was among the most magically potent of the [[Four Diminished Courts|four courts]] of the [[Fey Wild]]. Their power was rooted in **transformation** — metamorphosis as a literal magical principle, not a metaphor. This made them valuable to the Elven Empire, and dangerous enough that many were **exiled** rather than enslaved, pushed through [[The Hedge]] into the [[Mortal Plane]] where, without anchors of their own, they slowly diminished.

See also: [[On Faeries]], [[Talara]]

---

## Nature

The Chrysalis Court are deeply insect-coded. Their forms shift over lifetimes. In the Fey Wild they had access to the full cycle — larva, chrysalis, emergence. Cut off from the Fey, the cycle freezes. They are stuck between forms, forgetting what they were becoming.

The divine kinship between this court and [[Rethel]] — who holds Metamorphosis as a domain — is not accidental.

## Known Archetypes

### Moth Fae
The memory-keepers of the court. They hold the history of what the Chrysalis Court was — and cannot stop circling it. Grief is their gravity. They are drawn to moonlight and to flame, which ties them to both [[Sehanine]] and to [[Talara]]'s origins in the volcano cult.

### Cicada Fae
Ancient sleepers. Some have been dormant in the mortal realm since the fall of the Elven Empire, only now beginning to stir. They emerge disoriented and out of time, carrying memories of a world that no longer exists.

### Dragonfly Fae
The warriors and scouts. Before the Fey was locked, the dragonfly fae bridged between the [[Fey Wild]] and [[Elysium]] — the plane of dragons and drakona. They may carry intelligence about [[Sar'eth]] and the anchors that no one else possesses.

### Firefly Fae
The soul-guides. They light the way between life and death from the Fey side — the complement to the [[Aethris]], who guide souls through the [[Realms Below]]. [[Talara]]'s tribe, the **Lucitans**, were unknowingly serving these fae all along. When Talara finds the firefly fae, she is finding the origin of everything her people were.

## The Dead Tree

The Chrysalis Court's seat in the Fey Wild is a great **dead tree** — hollow, petrified, standing at the center of what was once the most beautiful clearing in the Fey Wild. It does not rot. It waits.

As Talara finds and records the scattered fae of the Chrysalis Court, something changes in the tree.

## The Lucitans

[[Talara]]'s tribe. They were tasked with maintaining a sacred fire deep in a volcano — a task whose true origin was in service to the firefly fae, though the Lucitans had long since forgotten this. When [[Sar'eth]] escaped, the purpose of the Lucitans was broken open.

This makes Talara's journey a **homecoming** she didn't know she was making.

---

## Status

Scattered across the mortal realm. The individual fae are diminished — some have lost their names, some their forms, many their memories. [[Talara]]'s role as Chronicler is to find them, record their true stories, and in doing so restore what was taken from them.

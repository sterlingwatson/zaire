# Yaaw

**Domains:** Magic • Mortality • The Unknown • The Three Realms
**Relationships:** Parent of [[Corvina]], creator of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]]
**Appearance:** In the old elven depictions, an elf with deep purple skin, orange eyes, and long white straight hair — ageless, an arbiter of magic. In the current era, depicted as an old or baby tortoise depending on context.
**Symbol:** 

---

## Overview

Yaaw is the god of mortality and magic — born from the corpse of [[The One]], and responsible for the creation of the three parallel realms: the [[Fey Wild]], the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]], and [[Elysium]]. These realms orbit the [[Mortal Plane]] and serve as both sources of magical power and destinations for souls after death.

Yaaw's nature is defined by the refusal to explain. They act, they create, they allow — but they do not justify or interpret. When [[Corvina]] was lost, Yaaw said nothing about it.

---

## Worship Under the Elven Empire

Worship or recognition of Yaaw was **forbidden** under the Elven Empire. The empire's theological position was that mortality was the punishment and justification for the lesser races' station in servitude — not a virtue, not a sacred function, not something to be honored. If Yaaw represented mortality as meaningful, then the mortal races were meaningful, and the empire's entire social order collapsed.

This prohibition extended by implication to [[Corvina]] and the [[The Aethris|Aethris]], whose work of guiding mortal souls suggested those souls had spiritual dignity. The persecution of the Aethris was ultimately a consequence of the Yaaw ban — they were practitioners of a theology the empire could not tolerate.

---

## Worship in the Free Kingdoms

After the fall of the empire, the prohibition loosened. In most of the free kingdoms, Yaaw is worshipped openly — the first month of the [[Zaire Calendar]], **Opalmoon**, is dedicated to Yaaw, associated with new beginnings, mortality, births, and the unknown.

But old taboos run deep. In some regions, particularly those with surviving elven cultural influence, Yaaw remains a god you acknowledge quietly rather than celebrate. The [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]]'s theology has its own complicated relationship with Yaaw's domains.

---

## The Three Realms

Yaaw created three realms from the divine corpse of [[The One]]:

- **[[Fey Wild]]** — realm of fae souls, anchored by the Elven Empire
- **[[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]]** — realm of mortal souls, now without guides
- **[[Elysium]]** — realm of draconic souls, carrying centuries of unresolved grief

See [[The Three Realms]] for the full orbital model and its consequences.

---

## Corvina

[[Corvina]] was Yaaw's daughter — a specific function of Yaaw's domain made autonomous. Her loss and reincorporation into Yaaw means that whatever Corvina was is now simply part of Yaaw again. Whether Yaaw is aware of what was Corvina in any recoverable sense is the kind of question Yaaw would not answer directly.

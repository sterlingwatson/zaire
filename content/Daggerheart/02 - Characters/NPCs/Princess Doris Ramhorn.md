# Princess Doris Ramhorn

## Basic Info
- **Location:** [[Kingdom of Kavittany]] [[Kempeir]]
- **Occupation/Role:** Princess, Captain of the Guard
- **Born:** 783
- **Died**:
- **Age:** 29
## Description
### Physical Appearance
- Human. Short blonde hair, often slightly messy hanging down into her light green eyes. About 5'7" with a medium frame. Often found in gleaming silver plate mail. On the breast plate the sigil of house Ramhorn, a goat with a fishes tail, stands brightly in gold. When she is not in armor, you can see the tight sinewing muscles in her arms

### Personality
- **Traits:** Renown swords woman. Uses a long sword both with one hand and two. She is gay, but keeps the information private. She is a free spirit and cares little what other think of her, she keeps here sexuality private for the sake of her father and sister. Forceful will. 
- **Mannerisms:** She has a recognizable smirk when she is challenged in a fight. So long as she is equipped with her sword her left hand will always be resting on the hilt.

## Backstory
- She grows up in Kavittany in a life of privilege being trained by all sort of courtiers. 
- Eventually she takes up fencing much to her father and mother dismay and excels in the art form. 
- Being older than Misty she would often help her sneak out and go running around the city. 
- As she grew older and her responsibilities changed she asked to be captain of the city guard, which she operates as now.

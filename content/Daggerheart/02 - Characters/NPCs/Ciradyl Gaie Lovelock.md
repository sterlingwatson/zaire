# Ciradyl Gaie Lovelock

## Basic Info

- **Faction/Group:** [[Kingdom of Kavittany]] [[Duchy of Touzi]]
- **Born:** 760
- **Died**: 792
- **Age:**
## Description
### Physical Appearance
- She is a beautiful #infernis with pale pink skin and short 4 inch horns which are straight, deep purple, and angled at a 60 degree angle backwards. She has bright yellow eyes and an oval face.  She wears her straight black hair tied in a bun behind her horns, when taken down it comes halfway down her back. She stands about 5'6". She has a slender build, but is very sinewy and strong from many hours spent fencing and dancing.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Agile, Graceful, Quiet, Soft
- **Mannerisms:** Smiles often despite her sharp teeth. Will stand on her tiptoes.
- **Speech Patterns:** Speaks slowly, Common was not her first language

## Backstory
- When she was 20 years old living in the [[Duchy of Touzi]] in the [[Jade Empire]] as the second daughter to [[Torpor Gaie]] Duke of Touzi, she met [[Maximus Lovelock II]]. He was a minor noble from a far away kingdom who had set out to be a pirate in the [Sea of Grey]. He had come stopping on the dock of her city [Poti] to resupply, on that evening there was a dance recital and Cira and he caught each others eye. This started a romance as each time Max would come in he would sneak into the keep.
- One evening he asked her to join him on the sea. At first she said no as her father had arranged a marriage to another minor noble in the empire, but within a week she sends a letter to Max to pick her up. Which led to her being disinherited and cut off. Her father hiring mercenaries to hunt them down.
- She turned out to be very good at the pirates life. Quick, agile and deadly she would swing onto the deck of an opposing ship and quickly move to kill the captain outright. Often choosing not to fight a clean fight on strength she fought smart and dirty.
- Soon she was known as the "Banshee of the Grey" which led their ship to being able to take ships without a fight. All the while fighting the mercenaries tailing them.
- When Max has to return to Kavittany to inherit his father/brother's estate they both gave up the life of adventure. Soon after they had [[Misty]], within her first two years Cira came down with a significant illness. She spent the next few years raising Misty but becoming quickly exhausted.
- Many times she feel so much doubt having left the sea, but she also loved Misty and Max so much. Passing in 792 she was buried in the Lovelock Mausoleum though she asked Max to one day return her family crest to the [[Sky Dragon Temple]] in the [[Jade Empire]]

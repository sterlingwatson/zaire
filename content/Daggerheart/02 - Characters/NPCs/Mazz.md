# Mazz

---

## Overview

Mazz was an [[The Aethris|Aethris]] agent — one of [[Corvina]]'s mortal representatives — who became the mortal half of [[The Hooded One]]. His merger with his [[The Thornwood Court|Thornwood Court]] fae patron created something neither of them fully intended: a fusion of righteous vengeance and fae power that has grown past its original purpose.

The severing of Mazz's connection to Corvina during this merger may have been the final break that caused Corvina's loss.

See also: [[The Hooded One]], [[Corvina]], [[The Aethris]], [[The Thornwood Court]]

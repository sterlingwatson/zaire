# Torris

---

## Overview

Torris is an [[The Aethris|Aethris]] cleric in the Legendary Heroes bonus campaign who takes the guiding of souls with particular seriousness. As an Aethris who uses the Dread domain, Torris represents [[Corvina]]'s dual nature — the soul-guide aspect, the one who carries the dead onward.

The mortal side of the unguided dead problem — what happens when there are no Aethris left to guide souls through the [[Hushed Lands]] — may surface through Torris's story.

See also: [[Corvina]], [[The Aethris]], [[The Legendary Heroes]]

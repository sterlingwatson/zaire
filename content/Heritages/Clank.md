# Clank

## Overview

Clanks are constructed beings — mechanical, magical, or some combination thereof. They were made, not born, and the question of who made them and why varies wildly. Some are ancient automatons from the Elven Empire. Some are recent creations of dwarven artificers. Some simply woke up one day with no memory of their origin.

## Culture in Zaire

Clanks have no unified culture. Each is defined by their maker's intent, their own accumulated experience, and the community that chose to accept them — or didn't. In [[Solira]], attitudes toward Clanks vary by region.

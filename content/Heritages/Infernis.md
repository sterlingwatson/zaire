# Infernis

## Overview

Infernis (sometimes called demons by outsiders) are mortal-rooted peoples connected to the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]]. The [[Jade Empire]] was founded in response to an Infernis invasion from the north (IR 3272 / FR 134), which shapes how Infernis are perceived across eastern [[Solira]].

## Culture in Zaire

Infernis culture varies dramatically between the Infernis of the [[Jade Empire]] (where select artificers produce [[Memory Stone]]s and other artifacts) and Infernis communities in western Solira.

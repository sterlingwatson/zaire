# Aethris

## Overview

The Aethris were the mortal agents of [[Corvina]] — created to guide souls into and out of the [[Mortal Plane]] on her behalf. They were not immortal. They were something between mortal and divine: beings shaped for a specific sacred function whose presence in the mortal realm was always temporary by design. In the current era, **the Aethris are gone** — Corvina has been lost and reincorporated into [[Yaaw]]. See [[The Aethris]] and [[Corvina]] for the full history.

## Culture in Zaire

Aethris culture was defined by function. Each individual's aura was colored to mirror one of Corvina's six birds (stork, magpie, pelican, raven, starling, dove) depending on their assigned task. The Elven Empire persecuted the Aethris specifically because their work implied mortal slaves had souls worthy of an afterlife. In the Legendary Heroes campaign (IR 3137), Aethris PCs still exist. Their domains are **Splendor** and **Dread**.

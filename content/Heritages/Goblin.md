# Goblin

## Overview

Goblins in the [[Mortal Plane]] are not the goblins of the [[Fey Wild]]. Generations of diaspora have made them something distinct — communities shaped by displacement, preservation, and ingenuity. See [[On Goblins]] for the full picture. The [[The Hollow Court|Hollow Court]] is their ancestral fey court.

## Culture in Zaire

Goblin culture in Solira is defined by survival and adaptation. They bore the worst of the elven slave trade, taken in greater numbers than any other fey-rooted people. The connection between mortal-realm goblins and the Hollow Court is cultural and ancestral, not direct. Most living goblins have never seen the Fey Wild.

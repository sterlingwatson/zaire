### Relentless (X) #passive #spotlight #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** This adversary can be spotlighted up to X times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them. **Flavor:** _Exceptionally dangerous and active, dominating the battlefield through sheer presence and capability_

### Pack Tactics #passive #combat #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Passive  
**Effect:** +1 to attack rolls when an ally is within Close range of the target **Flavor:** _Coordinates with allies for flanking attacks and tactical positioning_

### Frenzy #passive #combat #bloodied #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** When this adversary reaches their Major Threshold, they gain +1 to all rolls for the rest of the encounter **Flavor:** _Pain and desperation drive them to fight with reckless abandon_

### Armored Hide #passive #defensive #tier1 #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** +2 Armor Rating against physical damage **Flavor:** _Thick natural armor, reinforced carapace, or magically hardened skin_

### Magic Resistance #passive #defensive #magical #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** +2 to all reactions against magic effects and spells **Flavor:** _An innate resistance to magical energies that makes spells slide away ineffectively_

### Regeneration (X) #passive #healing #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** At the start of each GM turn, recover X HP (doesn't work if at 0 HP) **Flavor:** _Wounds close rapidly, flesh knits back together before your eyes_

### Fear Aura #passive #fear-gen #social #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** When first seen, all PCs within Close range must make Will + Intellect (12) or GM gains 1 Fear per failure **Flavor:** _An oppressive presence that fills nearby enemies with dread and uncertainty_

### Blur #passive #defensive #mobility #magical #tier2

**Type:** Passive  
**Effect:** Attacks against this adversary have disadvantage unless the attacker can see through illusions **Flavor:** _The creature appears to flicker and shift, making it difficult to target precisely_

## Combat Actions

### Cleave Attack #action #combat #melee #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Make a standard attack. If it hits, deal half damage to one other target within Close range of the original **Flavor:** _A devastating swing that carries through to strike multiple foes_

### Stunning Strike #action #combat #control #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Standard attack + target must make Might + Will (14) or lose their next action **Flavor:** _A precise blow that disrupts the target's ability to act_

### Breath Weapon #action #combat #area #fear-gen #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** All targets in a Close range cone take 2d6+4 damage (Agility + Intellect 14 for half). GM gains 1 Fear per target hit. **Flavor:** _Unleashes a devastating cone of elemental energy from its maw_

### Charge Attack #action #combat #mobility #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Move up to Far range and make an attack with +2 damage. Cannot be used if already within Close range of a target. **Flavor:** _Builds momentum for a devastating rushing attack_

### Multi-Attack (X) #action #combat #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Make X attacks against the same target or different targets within range **Flavor:** _Multiple limbs, weapons, or supernatural speed allows for several rapid strikes_

### Disarming Strike #action #combat #control #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Standard attack + target must make Agility + Will (12) or drop their weapon/lose grip on held item **Flavor:** _A precise strike that sends weapons flying from opponents' hands_

## Area Effects & Control

### Suppressing Blast #action #area #fear-gen #magical #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Mark Stress. Target a group within Far range. All targets make Agility + Intellect (14) or take 1d8+2 magic damage. Gain 1 Fear per target who marked HP. **Flavor:** _A burst of energy that overwhelms multiple opponents simultaneously_

### Web Spray #action #area #control #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Action  
**Effect:** Target area within Far range. All creatures in area make Agility + Might (12) or become Restrained until they spend an action to break free. **Flavor:** _Sticky strands coat the area, entangling anyone caught within_

### Intimidating Roar #action #social #fear-gen #area #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** All enemies within Far range make Will + Intellect (13) or GM gains 1 Fear per failure. Can only be used once per encounter. **Flavor:** _A bone-chilling sound that strikes terror into the hearts of all who hear it_

### Smoke Cloud #action #area #control #concealment #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Create a Close range area of thick smoke. All creatures within have disadvantage on attacks and cannot target anything outside the cloud. **Flavor:** _Dense, choking smoke that obscures vision and makes precise actions nearly impossible_

### Earthquake Stomp #action #area #control #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** All creatures within Close range make Agility + Might (15) or fall prone and take 1d6 damage. **Flavor:** _A tremendous impact that shakes the very ground beneath everyone's feet_

## Defensive Reactions

### Parry and Riposte #reaction #defensive #counterattack #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Reaction **Effect:** When missed by a melee attack, immediately make a standard attack against the attacker **Flavor:** _Deflects the blow and instantly counters with a precise strike_

### Shield Bash #reaction #defensive #control #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Reaction **Effect:** When attacked in melee, attacker makes Might + Will (12) or is pushed back one range category **Flavor:** _Slams shield into attacker, driving them backward_

### Spell Reflection #reaction #defensive #magical #tier3

**Type:** Reaction **Effect:** When targeted by a spell, make Will + Intellect (15). On success, reflect the spell back at the caster. **Flavor:** _Magical energies bounce off like light from a mirror_

### Damage Reduction #reaction #defensive #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Reaction **Effect:** When taking damage, reduce it by 3 (after armor). Can be used X times per encounter. **Flavor:** _Supernatural toughness or magical protection absorbs much of the impact_

### Phase Step #reaction #defensive #mobility #magical #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Reaction **Effect:** When targeted by an attack, become incorporeal until the end of the attacker's turn. Attack automatically misses. **Flavor:** _Briefly shifts out of phase with reality, becoming untouchable_

## Support & Utility

### Leadership #passive #support #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Allied adversaries within Far range gain +1 to all rolls **Flavor:** _Commands respect and coordination from nearby allies through tactical expertise_

### Healing Aura #passive #support #healing #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** At start of GM turn, all allied adversaries within Close range recover 2 HP **Flavor:** _Radiates restorative energy that mends wounds of nearby allies_

### Rally Cry #action #support #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Choose one allied adversary within Far range. They immediately take an action out of turn order. **Flavor:** _A commanding shout that spurs allies into immediate action_

### Summon Minions #action #support #fear-gen #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Spend 2 Fear to summon 1d3 Tier 1 adversaries of appropriate type. They appear within Close range. **Flavor:** _Calls forth additional combatants through magic, leadership, or hidden reserves_

### Tactical Reposition #action #support #mobility #tier2

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Choose up to 3 allied adversaries within Far range. They may each move one range category without provoking opportunity attacks. **Flavor:** _Coordinates a tactical repositioning that catches enemies off-guard_

## Special Movement

### Flying #passive #mobility #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Can move freely in three dimensions. Gains +2 to avoid area effects that target ground level. **Flavor:** _Soars through the air with wings, magic, or supernatural ability_

### Burrow #passive #mobility #ambush #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Passive  
**Effect:** Can move underground. When emerging to attack, gain +2 to the attack roll and +1 Fear if successful. **Flavor:** _Tunnels through earth and stone, emerging to catch prey unaware_

### Shadow Step #action #mobility #magical #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Teleport to any location within Far range that has shadows or darkness **Flavor:** _Dissolves into shadow and reappears elsewhere in an instant_

### Wall Crawling #passive #mobility #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Can move on walls and ceilings at normal speed. Gains advantage on ambush attacks from unusual angles. **Flavor:** _Scales surfaces like a spider, attacking from unexpected directions_

## Magical & Supernatural

### Arcane Steel #passive #magical #damage-type #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** All attacks deal both physical and magic damage **Flavor:** _Weapons and natural attacks are infused with mystical energy_

### Spell Immunity (Type) #passive #defensive #magical #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Completely immune to one school/type of magic (fire, charm, necromancy, etc.) **Flavor:** _Absolute protection against specific magical energies or effects_

### Magic Weapon Requirement #passive #defensive #magical #tier3

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Can only be damaged by magical weapons or spells. Non-magical attacks deal no damage. **Flavor:** _Supernatural protection that renders mundane weapons useless_

### Possession #action #magical #control #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Target makes Will + Intellect (16) or becomes controlled by the GM for 1 round. Can only target creatures at 0 HP or Major Threshold. **Flavor:** _Invades the target's mind and body, puppeteering their actions_

### Life Drain #action #magical #healing #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Action **Effect:** Standard attack + recover HP equal to damage dealt **Flavor:** _Siphons life force from victims to restore its own vitality_

## Environmental & Conditional

### Amphibious #passive #environmental #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Passive **Effect:** Can breathe air and water. Moves at full speed in aquatic environments. **Flavor:** _Equally at home on land or in water_

### Cold Immunity #passive #environmental #tier2 #tier3

**Type:** Passive  
**Effect:** Immune to cold damage and cold-based effects **Flavor:** _Supernatural resistance to freezing temperatures and ice magic_

### Sunlight Sensitivity #weakness #environmental #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Weakness **Effect:** Has disadvantage on all rolls while in direct sunlight **Flavor:** _Burns and weakens in the light of the sun_

### Blood Frenzy #conditional #combat #tier1 #tier2

**Type:** Conditional **Effect:** +2 to all attacks against targets who have taken damage this encounter **Flavor:** _The scent of blood drives it into a savage hunting frenzy_

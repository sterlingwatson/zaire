# Session {{session_number}} - The Great Hive

**Date:** 2025-08-16
**Players Present:** [[Wart]] [[Talara]] [[Abeiva]] [[Misty]] [[Klix]]
**Duration:** Long, Overtime

## Session Summary
Our heroes wake early at the priory to find that [[Borth]] had come to collect them and guide them to the Great Hive. Without much convincing the heroes decided to go along travel to the Great Hive.

As they hiked up the mountain while trying to make a place the notice [[Queen Sphesia]] standing on her balcony of the Great Hive. [[Borth]] advises them to bow when they enter the main chamber.

In the chamber our heroes meet [[Queen Sphesia]] in all of her resplendent glory. After some introductions the party is divided up to meet with or explore the hive. While Queen Sphesia uses here powers to practically force [[Abeiva]] to join here in her chambers.

In her chambers Sphesia offers Abeiva a deal to take a silk shirt from [[Nid]] which was a very powerful magic artifact in exchange for trying to convince their party to leave the Hive and return to settlements to express that everything was okay in the Buzzing Jungle. Abeiva under duress agreed to the offer, inadvertently agreeing to "carry a part of me with you" by Sphesia's own words. 

[[Misty]] under the effect of a minor charm was lead to [[Daria]]'s quarters in the Hive, where [[Daria]] seemed to know much more that what Misty had ever told her. In a conversation which was serving as a diversion for Daria to pass a letter to Misty which contained a key and information that speaking was not the best course of action because the Queen could hear everything.

[[Klix]] had a conversations with [[Albric]] in the great hall. Klix wasn't able to get much information but Albric didn't express outright hostility, though they were hard to read, at the old side still seems rather kindly though defiantly a vampire.

[[Talara]] and [[Wart]] went down to the honey factory with [[Erris Othel]] to see if they could find [[Alma Blackgrove]]. A drone brought Alma up who had already begun dronification, her eyes already changing into compound eyes. Wart, using his knowledge was able to hit a critical to understand immediately that this was some sort of necromancy. Talara speaking to her god [[Sehanine]] was able to find out that this was some sort of magic that [[Soulkilled]]. They ended up having to cast reassuaracne on Erris and tell her that Alma was completely gone, that her soul had been destroyed and they wouldn't be able to see each other even in the afterlife. 

After this everyone was taken to quarters to wait for dinner to start. While locked inside Misty shared information that speaking was dangerous and they communicated with letters. In their planning they decided to ambush the queen in the evening after the drones had gone to bed. 

During dinner [[Queen Sphesia]] used her knowledge network to try and stress the party out. She used her Command ability to force Misty, Wart, and Talara to eat some of the [[Blood Honey]]. Sphesia then brought in Erris to show the players that she had a hostage.

Back in the chambers Wart noticed a letter from Erris where she thanked the party for everything and that she was sorry to have gotten them into this mess. In the evening the heroes sprung their plan. Wart, Talara, and Misty would move to the balcony to try to get into Sphesia's chambers before she could do anything. Klix and [[Abeiva]] attempted to distract [[Borth Adversary]] and [[Tha'pal]] in the main room. Talara tried to fly up to the second level balcony with Wart be inevitably dropped him alerting the guards on the main level. It was then all hell broke loose. 

Upstairs the Queen was ambushed by Talara and Misty, to pretty good success, including an absolutely great combo attack where they dealt the queen a severe wound. Causing her to flee to her inner sanctum dropping her weapons. They joined in pursuit. Upon taking hostile action against the Queen a sharp pain went into Abeiva's arm and a read tattoo was made there.

Below [[Abeiva]] and [[Klix]] got stuck in with some merc gaurds. [[Tha'pal]] made some critical sneak attacks versus Wart. Klix and Abeiva did an admirable job with the mercs. Wart made an effort to isolate Borth in a fog cloud and try to convince her based on their connection to leave the battle. Borth was largely unconvinced.

In the inner sanctum Talara and Misty where greeted by a monsterous version of [[Queen Sphesia (Phase 2)]] as she morphed into her true form, a giant humanoid wasp. As they close in on her Erris tried to create an opening as was summarily killed and partially eaten by Queen Sphesia. [[Queen Sphesia (Phase 2)]] also opened up all of her tricks to try and kill her attackers.

Meanwhile the party upstairs was able to grind through some of the sell swords and make their way upstairs to reach the inner sanctum. Wart seeing the glamor having fallen away from the hive tries again to convince Borth, this time blinding them both in a fog cloud, this time making some progress.

As Klix and Abeiva reach the inner sanctum [[Queen Sphesia]] tries to finish them off, successfully pushing Abeiva to the edge of life and death as she stabs them all the way through. Abieva in that moment only sees the being that has been haunting their dreams. Klix heals Abieva though allowing them to stand up and channeling their raw power to blast the queens head off.

Back in the chambers [[Wart]] has convinced Borth to leave the combat. As she is leaving she imparts information to Wart that there is a secret passage into the mountains, yet she is not able to break her word and attack the queens forces. 

Regrouping upstairs to finish off [[Tha'pal]] the heroes are battered but she will not give up. Eventually [[Tha'pal]] is able to kill Wart. In that moment between life and death Wart sees his childhood friend [[Tabatha]] pointing up into the constellations telling the story of Wart as a hero like [[Bartholomew]]. Wart chooses not to die though and is later healed. 

[[Abeiva]] again is able to finish Tha'pal with a rapier to the neck finishing the fight. As dawn begins to rise fireworks go off from the jungle signaling an [[Akavarii]] attack. Our heroes grab the loot and head for the secret passage where we find ourselves now.

# Session {{005}} - Kempier Romp

**Date:** 2025-09-27
**Players Present:** [[Gearson]] [[Klix]] [[Misty]] [[Abeiva]] [[Wart]] [[Talara]]
**Duration:** Appox 3 hours

## Session Summary

Our heroes wake up in the city Kempier Capitol of Kavittany and Misty's home town. After a breakfast at the Lovelock home with Maximus and [Tabitha] with letters passed around our heroes split up to explore the city.

The gang all went down the the [[Kempeir Wharf]] to get paid by the [[Iron Eels]] the local theives guild that the did the orginal job for. With this money some of our hero went shopping!

[[Misty]] walked with [[Talara]] off to meet [[Daria]] at the temple to Ennem. She met up with Daria and got a few more answers, but nothing was really set firm. She did find that Daria and her new crew had at least some parallel interests. Misty headed off for a meeting later in the day with [[Vexsalu Tzikma]].

[[Wart]] was going to head off the the [Hotel de Hero] to pay his repects to [[99 - Archive/Bartholomew]] where he met [[B.A.R.T.]] (Bard Automota for Reducing Tyranny) who doubted Wart's commitment to living up to [[99 - Archive/Bartholomew]]'s ideal challenging him to a duel of good deeds, which Wart took him up on. Through the rest of the session we had vinettes of their competition which ended in a tie.

[[Talara]] went off to the temple to [[Sehanine]] where she gain divine insight into her quest. Though upon leaving she was confronted by a mysterious Lucitan like her who immediately flew in to attack her. She was accused of abandoing her people in their greatest need, and he had to scarific everything to protect them. He claimed to be responsible for her parents survival. [[Talara]] choose not to fight him and instead flew off. Once the gaurds caught wind the attacker fled. [[Talara]] spent the rest of the day reflecting.

[[Gearson]] went off to follow some leads on finding his sister [[Lore]]. Ultimately ending up at [[Kempeir Explore's Guild]] where he met the cowardly guild leader [[Storm]] addressing a worried crowd of commoner demanding that the Explorers do something now that [[Sar'eth]] is back. Gearson, through naivety or lack of social awareness ended up further humiliating him. After the crowd dispersed Gearson had a chat and ended up joining the explorers guild just as his sister had. He was also told that Lore would be back later in the day, so he waited. When she got back they caught up and Gearson leaned that Lore had joined up with her own adventuring party so she could get out in the world and explore the different lives of goblins in Zaire.

[[Abeiva]] had a lead on a tailor who may be able to get them some new duds and possibly remove part of the curse in the shirt. After a chat the tailor asked him to come back after hours. Abeiva then chose to go chill at a local tavern where he had some apple hand pies and cider. But was interrupted by [[Orpa]] an eccentric simia monk type who appeared to know Abeiva from the [Jade Empire]. Abeiva on the way to the tailor was also jumped by two agents of the Empire who seems to want them dead. Orpa jumped in to the rescue. At the tailor abeiva's shirt was removed of the curse allow it to be taken off, but [[Queen Sphesia]] still remained in their head.

[[Klix]] had a meeting with his old friend [[Grit]] at his apothecary where they got to have a nice time catching up. Klix learned the recipe for a minor health potion and minor stamina potion. (Give the mechanics also give a rumor. But as the afternoon went on something strange all of a sudden happened. Grit's eyes went black and he accused Klix of being unable to save [[Akal]] one of his former students/children. Klix got three rolls to mitigate what was about to happen, failing all three. Grit turned into a pile of beetles, centipeds, and grubs and the apothecary exploded in dark arcane energy. Luckly he survived and his friend [[Wart]] rushed up.

[[Misty]] had her meeting with [[Vexsalu Tzikma]] where he asked about the events at the [[Buzzing Jungle Hive]]. Misty filled him in. He let her know about the political situation in Akavariia getting worse. And about the [[Lord Protector Morcwell]]. Then a bright burst of light from a phosphorus firework goes off in the courtyard and Akavarii Samuri attack to find and kill the "pretender". In the confusion [[Maximus Lovelock II]] finds Misty and Vex. Telling them to go to the Estate and find his old ship, that Uncle Hael will know where to find it. He then defends them as they escape to a small boat heading up the river.

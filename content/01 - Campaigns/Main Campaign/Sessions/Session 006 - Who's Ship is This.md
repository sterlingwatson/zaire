# Session 006 - Who's Ship is This

**Date:** 2025-11-08
**Players Present:** [[Wart]]  [[Klix]] [[Gearson]] [[Misty]] [[Talara]]
**Duration:** 3hrs

## Session Summary
Our heroes traveled to the [[Lovelock Estate]] upon arriving meeting "Uncle" [[Hael Tripor]] who greeted them and let them know where [[Maximus Lovelock II]]'s ship the [[Orca]] was.

They headed off on a cliffside trail, splashed by sea spray. [[Abeiva]] stayed back at the estate believing it to be a simple fetch quest. He enjoyed some of the Lovelock family wine and enjoyed the views of the sea.

At the cove where the the ship was our heroes hear some folks talking and attempting to steal the Orca. After a long time debating the bandits dropped the sails and our heroes rushed in. 

Asked them their favorite parts of the fight.

After the end of the fight and showing mercy on a confused dwarf [[Misty]] went into the captains quarters where she found a few letters of her father's and a capitan's hat.

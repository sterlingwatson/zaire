# Sar'eth

---

## Overview

Sar'eth is the last surviving dragon — and the last being alive who remembers the drakona name for the continent: **Vyremor** — the one who absorbed the grief and power of an entire civilization's erasure during [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt. He survived the extermination, held space for the fragmented draconic spirits in the volcano for eight hundred years, and made a deal with [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]] at the end of the Campaign of Broken Chains.

The public story is that Bartholomew locked the dragon away. The truth is stranger and kinder than the legend.

In the main campaign, **Sar'eth has left the volcano.** The Lucitan cult is broken. What he intends to do now — and what [[Talara]]'s role is in restoring the balance he asked for — is the central arc of the campaign.

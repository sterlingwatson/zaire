### Speeches/Lore given

 - I need to come clean with you all.
 - Traveling to the [[Commonwealth]] will be dangerous, it is patrolled by the forces of Pontifex [[Corippos]], he took over political control of the Commonweath when I was a teenager.
 - My father Karst, after the lose of my mother Rilla in childbirth, lost his way. He blamed our ancestors, our gods for losing his one. 
 - In the [[Forsynth Desert]] there is a people who call themselves the [[Tafful]], they worship a god called [[Tafulet]] who is singular and all powerful. From this people came a  prophet named [[Corippos]] who came to the commonwealth and started the [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]]. He preached a radical message, that the old ways had left the Commonwealth behind.
 - In that time the Commonwealth had lost a series of conflicts with the Jade Empire, so much so that the military was unable to man the boarder stations on the [[Forest of Blackest Woes]] and the [[Forsynth Desert]], which allowed terrifying beasts to run amok in the soft inner parts of the duchies that make up [Lietuva].
 - My father, leader of the [[Rockbiter Clan]] one of the 6 major clans of Lietuva, in his personal loss was an early supporter of Corippos. And so the [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]] spread rapidly.
 - Our people left the old ways.
 - About 7 years ago the old queen [[Camirsa]] died. In the Commonwealth, when a ruler passes a Witan is called where a new Ruler is selected by clan leaders and nobles of the realm. Though he did not have a rightful claim [[Corippos]] was elected, largely through the support of my father and the nobles of Pskovy along the boarder with the [[Jade Empire]].
 - In the early years things we honestly a bit better, the religious fervor led to a large military build up and a reversal of resent losses. The border stations where remanned and peace largely returned.
 - Then as things became peaceful [[Corippos]] siezed more and more power, his cult became everything. Its dogma became authoritarian, centralizing, and in someway apocalyptic. 
 - They viewed all other gods as false idols, aspects of Sol which had been broken off. They see the collapse of the Elven Empire as the first step in dismantling their false beliefs and that [[Sar'eth]] must return as an aspect of Sol to finish what it had started.
 - I must go back to try and slow this process, to over throw the tyranny that has taken over my home. I tell you this not to ask for your help, but as a warning, following me on this path would be dangerous and deadly. Though one day I would love help in this matter I think you should focus on getting stronger.

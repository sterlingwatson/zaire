## Description
### Physical Appearance
- Balding man with a grey half ring of hair adorning his scalp. He wear a modest but beautiful crown encrusted with sapphires. A handsome doublet hugs his breast and a white cape with black adorments lays upon his back. He walks with a limp, stands about 5 ft 9. He feels the cold much more than he ever had these days.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Kindly, but serious. Starting to lose mental acuity. Puts a lot of faith in his advisors.
- **Mannerisms:** He speak deliberately making sure to make deep eye contact. We he walks he attempt to hid the pain he feels in the limp but will occasionally grimace. 
### Motivations
- **Wants:** To ensure the safety of the realm. Avoid the kind of chaos that led to him rising to power.

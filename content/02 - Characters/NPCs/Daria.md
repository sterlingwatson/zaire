## Relationships
### With PCs
- **[[Misty]]**: Misty was paid by her to smuggle some illegal goods into the city and deliver it to a house. She was advised by Daria to not look inside of the package, which she didn't; if it was something against Misty's morals, she would rather not try her luck. After it was successfully delivered, Daria and Misty began to write to each other with Daria asking every so often for Misty to deliver another package. Soon it became less of a business deal and more of a favor for a friend. 
- 
- Although they did not share personal secrets or confide in each other, it was a relationship built on trust and mutual respect. Misty likes that Daria takes her seriously and sees that she's smarter than she lets on. After a few months of delivering for Daria, Misty discovered the packages were going a home who housed Daria's infant son, taken care of by a nanny. She still doesn't dare open the packages for the sake of breaking their trust. Misty hasn't received a letter from Daria in about a month, but this is normal. Her letter are unpredictable, but there is never a break longer than 2.5 months between them.

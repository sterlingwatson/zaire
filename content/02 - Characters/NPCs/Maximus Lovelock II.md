## Backstory
- Born the second son to the Duke Maximus Lovelock Max was never expected to inherit the Duchy. He spend much of his time sparring, sailing, traveling, and enjoying life as a minor noble. This led to both his elder brother and father to be irked by his behavior. He was always a momma's boy though, yet she passed when he was in his late teens.
- This leads his to take his ship off to find a pirating vessel what he found was a whaling baliger which he names the [[Orca]]. He makes some modifications with most of his remaining money to increase its speed and hires a small crew. Setting out for the Grey Sea, the shallow sea in between the [[Jade Empire]] [[Duchy of Lortia]] and [[Barony of Blanc]] as there was much trade and adversarial relationships to exploit. With a letter of marque given to him by the [[Kingdom of Gormly]] he set out on adventure.
- To mixed success at first. Max was deadly with a warhammer and a crack sailor, he just tended to have to fight each ship he tried to take. On a resupply run in the [[Jade Empire]] city of [Poti] he met [[Ciradyl Gaie Lovelock]] at one of her dance performances.
- They were fast in love after 6 months of sneaking over the castle walls Max asked her to join him. She originally refused but eventually joined.
- Together they became a terror in the Grey Sea. Appearing quickly out of the fog, closing the distance, and boarding in a matter of minutes. Cira able to incapacitate the captian within seconds, they were able to take ships with out a fight.
- After 3 months at sea they were married in a ceremony on the Orca's deck. Cira in the sky blue traditional of the [[Jade Empire]] and he in the red of [[Asel]] they were united as one. There is a [[Memory Stone]] hidden in the captain's quarters of the [[Orca]]
- Eventually Max's father died and his brother [[Albert Lovelock]] inherits the estate. Albert writes to his brother pleading for him to come home, especially worried about the state of the kingdom which had fallen into anarchy at the overthrow of a tyrant king which was preceded by the [[Blood Comet]]. Sadly in 784 Albert is killed in battle against a rival house and Max inherits the land. After much arguments and soul-search he and Cira return to Kavittany, to the [[Lovelock Estate]]
- Max feeling a need for revenge joins the future King Wisel in a campaign to install a compromise candidate as king and arrest the rival house that killed his brother. He proved himself to be a stratigic commander, leader of men, and judge of character. Pirating had given him a wide array of skills. At Wisel's side they were able to calm the kingdom. Shortly after the end of the rebellion [[Misty]] was born and the family was called to [[Kempeir]] to serve on the council. 
- They enjoyed a pleasant life until when [[Misty]] was about 2 her mother became very ill, eventually passing in 792.
- Max was crushed and feel into a deep depression. He had been relieved of duties while she was ill and he remained away for years afterwards. In 795 he starts to feel better and in that fall he returns to Kempier taking Misty with him.
- He never tells Misty about his past, for the most part she thinks him a boring noble. 
- He has kept the Orca in a cave along the coast at the estate.
- In his youth a local count's son named [[Hael Tripor]] often sparred with Max and they were close friends. He ended up becoming the first mate of [[Orca]] and eventually he was given the task in managing the [[Lovelock Estate]].

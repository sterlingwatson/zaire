### Backstory
**Description:** Blue skin, tall, 

**Background:** Abeiva was born and raised in a place called the [[Jade Empire]] - a society with a focus on extreme discipline and power dynamics. He was an #imperial #soldier for their empire, even participating in war. Abeiva was soon to be married to their fiance, [[Sade]], on a short vacation back home when a terrible accident occurred.

One evening, during a hunting trip in the rain, he was viciously attacked by an unknown creature and almost died. He woke up bleeding on the ground when a rescue party hound him. He was alive, but had no recollection of his life prior to the attack.

Because of his amnesia and the attack, [[Abeiva]] was forced into an early retirement. For months his fiancée and family tried to bring his memories back, but to no avail. Soon after the incident, [[Abeiva]] began suffering repeating, vivid nightmare of a woman with cold, blue eyes strangling him to death, Not only that, but strange powers began to imprint on them that they couldn't control, further distancing his family away.

It was then decided by their superior: [[Abeiva]] would be exiled until they can control themselves and get their memories back.

After leaving, [[Abeiva]] goes on a journey to find the woman in his dreams, in hopes that she can give him some answers to his troubles.

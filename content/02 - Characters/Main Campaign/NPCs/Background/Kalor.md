## Description
### Physical Appearance
- Very Old Galapa. 4'11". Hunched over. The scales on his arm are adorn about every third one with a sunset orange scale that gleams. His shell is decorated with tessalating triangles of pink blues and oranges. He has a sweet toothless smile.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Kindly, Hospitable, Smart
- **Mannerisms:** Calls everyone a kid, wippersnapper etc.
- **Speech Patterns:** Slow, deliberate

### Motivations
- **Wants:** To graden and live out the rest of his day in the jungle of his childhood. To grow many flowers and to pass watching the painted evening sky fade to purples and indegos.
- **Fears:** Losing his garden. Not ever seeing his children again. Earning the wrath of the Queen by spilling the beans about the honey.
- **Secrets:** Knows not to consume the honey

## Backstory
- Growing up in the Eastern region of Kavittany [[Vilois]], which is ruled by the Lovelock family Hael's family ruled a small county know for having great foraging lands in the forest on the border of [[Sivania]].
- He being a third son did not stand to inherit so spent most of his time at the [[Lovelock Estate]] at court. There he met Max and they became fast friends as boys.
- They sailed, spared and got into trouble.
- When make left for the adventuring life he took Hael along with him. Who became his first mate.
- Hael has a mind for administration and is a crack shot with a crossbow. He primarily served as the ship Boson. 
- He is who's point of view the marriage memory stone is made from as the participants wanted to be able to see each other.
- After they all returned to Kavittany Hael was left in charge of the estate in Max's absence, a job Hael is quite good at.
- He never married or had children. (He is gay)

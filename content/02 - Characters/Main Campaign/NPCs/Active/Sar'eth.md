# Sar'eth

---

## Overview

Sar'eth is the last surviving dragon, and the last being alive who remembers the drakona name for the continent: **Vyremor**,the one who absorbed the grief and power of an entire civilization's erasure during [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt. He survived the extermination, held space for the fragmented draconic spirits in the volcano for eight hundred years.

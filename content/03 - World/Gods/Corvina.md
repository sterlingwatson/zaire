# Corvina

**Relationships:** Daughter of [[Yaaw]]

---

## Overview

Corvina was a minor deity — daughter of [[Yaaw]], and responsible for guiding souls both into and out of the [[Mortal Plane]]. She created the [[Aethris]] to act as her agents among the living. She is depicted with wings, though their form shifts depending on her function.

In the present day of the main campaign, **Corvina is lost.** Her nature has been reincorporated back into [[Yaaw]]. The Aethris, without their patron, are gone as a people.

See also: [[Yaaw]], [[The Aethris]], [[The Three Realms]], [[The Hushed Lands]], [[The Hooded One]]

---

## Nature and Appearance

Corvina is depicted with bird wings, and the specific wings vary by role:

**As life-bringer** — carrying souls of new children into the world:
Stork wings, magpie wings, or pelican wings. She is depicted warmly in this aspect, often small-statured, practical, focused on the work.

**As soul-carrier** — guiding the dead onward:
Raven wings, starling wings, or dove wings. This aspect is neither grim nor cold — she is not a death goddess in the sense of dominion over death, but a guide. The passage is simply her work.

These are not two different beings or two different moods. They are the same function — she manages thresholds, and birth and death are the same threshold from different directions.

---

## The Aethris

Corvina created the [[Aethris]] to act as her agents on the Mortal Plane. They were not immortal — their presence in the mortal realm was described as *"a temporary lease from Corvina."* They were always meant to pass back through.

The Aethris' auras reflected Corvina's birds — each individual's aura was colored to mirror one of the six birds depending on the specific task Corvina gave them at their creation. At certain angles the wings could be seen spread across their backs in the light of their aura.

The Elven Empire persecuted the Aethris specifically because their work — guiding the souls of "lesser beings" into the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]] and onward — implied that those beings had souls worthy of an afterlife. This threatened the theological justification of the empire's entire structure. If mortal slaves had souls that passed into spiritual continuity, they were not lesser beings. The empire needed them to be lesser beings. The Aethris were therefore treated as dangerous heretics, hunted with the particular hatred reserved for ideas that cannot simply be ignored.

*Corvina herself was never banned. She was simply never spoken of. [[Yaaw]] worship being forbidden in the empire extended to her by implication.*

---

## Loss

How Corvina was lost is unresolved and worth deciding. Possibilities:

- The persecution of the Aethris severed enough of her connection to the Mortal Plane that she diminished and was absorbed back into Yaaw
- The anchoring of the Fey Wild disrupted the orbital cycle of the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]] enough to destabilize her function
- The Hooded One's warlock — [[Mazz]], her Aethris agent — had his connection to her severed during his merger with the [[Thornwood Court]] patron, and this was somehow the final break
- She chose to return to Yaaw when the Aethris were gone, because her purpose required agents she no longer had

What is certain is that in the current era, whatever Corvina was is now simply Yaaw. The souls of the newly dead who would have been guided by Aethris agents are not being guided. What is happening to them instead is an open question that no one in the main campaign has fully reckoned with yet.

---

## What Her Loss Means

The Lucitans guided fae souls to the [[The Great Tree|Great Tree]]. The Aethris guided mortal souls through the Hushed Lands. Both systems are now broken:
- The Lucitan system broke with the anchoring, leaving fae spirits fragmented
- The Aethris system broke with Corvina's loss, leaving mortal souls without guides

The world has two parallel problems of the unguided dead, one in the Fey and one in the mortal realm, and almost no one knows either problem exists. [[Talara]] will come to understand the fae side of this through her journey. The mortal side may be a later arc entirely, or may surface through [[Torris]], the Aethris cleric in the Legendary Heroes who took the guiding of souls with particular seriousness.

---

## Relationship to Yaaw

Corvina was Yaaw's daughter — a specific function of Yaaw's domain made autonomous, given form and agency. Her return to Yaaw is less like a death and more like a river rejoining the sea. Whether Yaaw is aware of what was Corvina in any recoverable sense is the kind of question Yaaw would not answer directly.

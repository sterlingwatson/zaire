# The Gilded Court

---

## Overview

The Gilded Court were the gnome court of the [[Fey Wild]]  earth-fae, deeply clever, and the most "civilized" by elven standards. It was this legibility to the Elven Empire that made them useful, and this usefulness that became their moral catastrophe.

They served as **brokers** for the elven slave trade. They knew the [[Fey Wild]] better than any elf ever could, and they used that knowledge to facilitate the taking of fae and goblins in exchange for protection and status.

See also: [[On Gnomes]], [[On Redcaps]], [[On Faeries]]

---

## What They Did

The Gilded Court were not simply complicit, they were **instrumental**. Without their knowledge of Fey geography, community locations, and which fae had powers requiring suppression, the Elven Empire could not have operated at the scale it did. They were the map.

They told themselves:
- The elves would leave eventually
- They were cushioning the worst of it
- Their people were safer with them at the table
- A seat at the table was leverage

They were wrong on all counts, and some of them knew it even then.

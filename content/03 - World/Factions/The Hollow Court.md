# The Hollow Court

---

## Overview

The Hollow Court is the goblin court of the [[Fey Wild]] — the most numerous of the [[Four Diminished Courts|four courts]], the most systematically targeted by the Elven Empire, and the least protected. Their current name is not their original one.

See also: [[On Goblins]], [[On Faeries]], [[The Hooded One]]

---

## Origins

Goblins were created by [[Yaaw]] alongside the faeries from the divine corpse of [[The One]]. They shared the [[Fey Wild]] with the fae courts from the beginning. They were not considered glamorous by the standards of the other courts — they were trickster-coded, chaotic, unglamorous. This made them easier to dismiss and easier to take.

The [[Gilded Court]] gnomes, who served as brokers for the Elven Empire, found the Hollow Court's communities first and most often. The goblins never forgot this.

---

## The Scale of the Taking

The Hollow Court bore the worst of the elven slave trade. They were taken in numbers that the other courts were not, in part because the Gilded Court gnomes knew exactly where to find them, and in part because no one fought hard enough to stop it.

## Status in the Fey Wild

The Hollow Court is fragmented. What remains in the [[Fey Wild]] is tribal, scattered, and surviving in the overgrowth of the corrupted [[Hedge]]. Many goblins followed [[The Hooded One]] — he was, genuinely, furious on their behalf. But his corruption of the Fey Wild is now killing them too.

He has become a liberator who burns down the house with the people still inside.

---

## Status in the Mortal Realm

The goblins of the mortal realm are not the same as the Hollow Court fae — generations of diaspora have made them something distinct. See [[On Goblins]] for the full picture of goblin life in the mortal realm.

The connection between mortal-realm goblins and the Hollow Court is **cultural and ancestral**, not direct. Most living goblins have never seen the Fey Wild. The Hollow Court exists for them as myth, history, and wound.

---

## The Hooded One Problem

[[The Hooded One]] claims to be the Hollow Court's avenger. Many goblins — both in the Fey and in the mortal realm — believe this. His origins are in genuine persecution; the [[Aethris]], like the goblins, were targeted by the Elven Empire for connecting "lesser beings" to spiritual dignity.

But his hatred has grown past the elves. The Hollow Court goblins who remain in the Fey Wild are caught between a corrupted homeland and a champion who is making it worse.

This creates a difficult situation for [[Talara]] and for any goblin player character: what do you do with a revolutionary who was right once and has since become something else?

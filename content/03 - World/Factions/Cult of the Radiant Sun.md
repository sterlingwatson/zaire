# The Cult of the Radiant Sun

---

## Overview

The Cult of the Radiant Sun is a religious and political movement that has grown over the centuries since the fall of the Elven Empire, slowly and then quickly occupying [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]]'s legacy. They claim spiritual descent from the Campaign of Broken Chains and have built a theology around the Legendary Heroes that serves their own interests.

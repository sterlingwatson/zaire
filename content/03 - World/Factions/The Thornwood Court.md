# The Thornwood Court


---

## Overview

The Thornwood Court is the oldest of the [[Four Diminished Courts|four courts]] and "court" is almost too structured a word for what they are. They predate the structure of courts entirely. They are what the [[Fey Wild]] **is** at its most primal: the residue of divine death given form, the part of [[Yaaw]]'s creation that never wanted to be named or organized.

Hags. Unicorns. The great speaking trees. Things with too many eyes and no clear shape. The Thornwood Court is all of these.

See also: [[On Faeries]], [[The Hooded One]], [[The Hedge]]

---

## Nature

The Thornwood Court and the Fey Wild are not fully separable. To corrupt the Fey Wild is to corrupt the Thornwood Court. To heal the Fey Wild is to heal the Thornwood Court. They are the same thing at the root.

This is why [[The Hooded One]]'s takeover of the Fey Wild has hit them hardest of all the courts his hatred is not contained to a part of the Fey, it infects the whole, and the Thornwood Court *is* the whole.

---

## The Patron

[[The Hooded One]]'s fae patron came from the Thornwood Court. She was an ancient being who had watched the elves lock the Fey Wild in place and had been seething ever since. Her pact made with [[Mazz]] allows her to take power from [[The Realms Below]] and ultimately mimic the powers of [[Corvina]]

---

## Current Status

The Thornwood Court is the most corrupted of the four courts. What was once primal and ancient and bounded is now **feral and unbounded:**

- Unicorns that were once healing creatures are hollow, antler-dark things
- Hags who were once cunning but bounded are now purely predatory
- The great speaking trees have gone silent or begun to speak in the Hooded One's voice

This court cannot be freed the way the [[Chrysalis Court]] can. It cannot have its stories recorded in the way that restores individual fae.

---

## The Hedge

The Thornwood Court has the deepest relationship with [[The Hedge]], the liminal space between the mortal realm and the Fey Wild. The Hedge was always their borderland. Its current grotesque overgrowth is in part a reflection of their corruption.

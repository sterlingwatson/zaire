# Infernis

## Overview

Infernis (sometimes called demons by outsiders) are mortal-rooted peoples connected to the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]]. The [[Jade Empire]] was founded by an Infernis invasion from the north (IR 3272 / FR 134), which shapes how Infernis are perceived across eastern [[Solira]].

## Appearance


## Culture in Zaire

Infernis culture varies dramatically between the Infernis of the [[Jade Empire]] and those still in the far North in the [[Demon's Crown]]. In the Jade Empire most of the infernis are highly centralized, bureaucratic and militaristic. We don't often find many infernis outside of the Jade Empire and if so they often operate as mercenaries or agents for the Empire. 

The continuous invasions from the North have cause 4 dynasties and will it 4 major sub-ancestries of infernis.

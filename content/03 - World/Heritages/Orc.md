# Orc

## Overview

Orcs were created by [[Comforce]], god of the seas, to be strong and fit for sailing. They are draconic-rooted, with their power resonating with [[Elysium]]. Despite their sea-god origin, orc communities in [[Solira]] are found both coastal and inland.

## Appearance

## Culture in Zaire

Orc culture retains strong maritime traditions in coastal communities, with Comforce worship centered around the month of **Seatide**. Inland orc communities have adapted their traditions but often maintain cultural connections to the sea.

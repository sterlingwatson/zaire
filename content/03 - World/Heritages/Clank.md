# Clank

## Overview

Clanks are constructed beings either mechanical, magical, or some combination thereof. They were made, not born, and the question of who made them and why varies wildly. Some are ancient automatons. Some are recent creations of [[Dwarf|dwaven]] or [[Goblin|gobish]] artificers. Some simply woke up one day with no memory of their origin.

## Appearance


## Culture in Zaire

Clanks have no unified culture. Each is defined by their maker's intent, their own accumulated experience, and the community that chose to accept them. In [[Solira]], attitudes toward Clanks vary by region, but are generally welcomed.

Gobish Clanks are often made of clay or wood, though through the [[Gobish Language]], which still contains the fey magic of creation, they are given remarkable life and are often jovial types. *For real life inpiration look to the Golems of Jewish culture*

Dwarven Clanks on the other hand are made of metal and infused with life with [[Dwarven Runes|dwarven runic magic]]. Typically these clanks are more hard labor oriented and hardworking. Similar to the dwarves themselves without the love of ale and drink.

Other Clanks in the world either come from a time long ago and have since lost many of their memories [[Kal Ral Ed|The Oracle of Fetege Monastery]] for instance. Or were almost accidental creations by other peoples, given life on the whim of a god or comparably powerful entity.

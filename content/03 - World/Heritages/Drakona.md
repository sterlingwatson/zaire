# Drakona

## Overview

The Drakona are the mortal descendants of dragonkind, the survivors of [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt, which destroyed 99% of all dragons. They carry the resonance of [[Elysium]] in their blood, and their power grows when that realm draws near the [[Zaire|Mortal Plane]]. In the current era, with the [[Fey Wild]] artificially anchored and Elysium distant, the Drakona are at diminished magical capacity a state that has persisted for over eight centuries. In our campaign we have yet to meet any [[Drakona]]

## Appearance

"Drakona resemble wingless dragons in humanoid form and possess a powerful elemental breath. All drakona have thick scales that provide excellent natural armor against both attacks and the forces of nature. They are large in size, ranging from 5 feet to 7 feet on average, with long sharp teeth. New teeth grow throughout a Drakona’s approximately 350-year lifespan, so they are never in danger of permanently losing an incisor. Unlike their dragon ancestors, drakona don’t have wings and can’t fly without magical aid. Members of this ancestry pass down the element of their breath through generations, though in rare cases, a drakona’s elemental power will differ from the rest of their family’s." - Daggerheart Core Rule Book
## Culture in Zaire

The Drakona remember what was done to them. [[Sar'eth]] survived; most did not. Drakona communities in [[Solira]] carry a deep cultural wound from the Great Hunt, and their relationship to elves, even the scattered elves of the current era, is complicated at best. They call the continent **Vyremor**.

# Goblin

## Overview

Goblins in the [[Mortal Plane]] are not the goblins of the [[Fey Wild]]. Generations of diaspora have made them something distinct, communities shaped by displacement, preservation, and ingenuity. See [[On Goblins]] for the full picture. The [[The Hollow Court|Hollow Court]] is their ancestral fey court.

## Culture in Zaire

Goblin culture in Solira is defined by survival and adaptation. They bore the worst of the elven slave trade, taken in greater numbers than any other fey-rooted people. The connection between mortal-realm goblins and the Hollow Court is cultural and ancestral, not direct. No living goblin has ever seen the Fey Wild. They are found in pretty much every major settlement in the south and east, tending to stay concentrated in their own neighborhoods. They are generally well-liked wherever they go as they are often very helpful, kind, and fabulous tinkers.

# Human

## Overview

Humans were created by [[Sehanine]] and [[Asel]] (Moon and Sun respectively) and are among the most numerous peoples on [[Solira]]. They are mortal-rooted, connected to the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]], and form a plurality population of most free kingdoms (Kavittany, Gormly, and Veche) while still making up small pockets in the north and east.

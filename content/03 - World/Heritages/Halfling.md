# Halfling

## Overview

Halflings are mortal-rooted peoples connected to the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]]. Their magical aptitude grows when that realm draws near the [[Mortal Plane]].

## Culture in Zaire

Halfling communities are common throughout the free kingdoms. They tend toward practical, community-oriented cultures with strong traditions of hospitality and mutual aid. Two large Halfling communities exist and have divergent cultures.

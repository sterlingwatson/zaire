# Firbolg

## Overview

Firbolg were created by [[Lorta]], god of plants, to be gentle cultivators of the land. They are fey-rooted and their power resonates with the [[Fey Wild]]'s proximity.

## Appearance


## Culture in Zaire

Firbolg communities are deeply tied to the natural world. They are among the most likely peoples to maintain direct relationships with druid circles. Their gentleness should not be mistaken for weakness. They are also know to be phenomenal farmers and maintain strong community identity.

You will find many Firbolgs in the great farmlands of [[Commonwealth of the Radiant Sun|Commonwealth]], [[Jade Empire]] and Eastern [[Kingdom of Gormly|Gormly]]. Though they are known to be found all over so long as their is good land to till.

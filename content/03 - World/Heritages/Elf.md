# Elf

## Overview

Elves were created by [[Sehanine]] and once ruled much of [[Solira]] through the Elven Empire. That empire anchored the [[Fey Wild]], conducted the Great Hunt against dragonkind, and enslaved fae and goblins for centuries before collapsing under the weight of the [[The Legendary Heroes|Campaign of Broken Chains]], internal plague, and the loss of their immortality. In the current era (FR 812), elves are **rare**. Encountering one is notable.

## Culture in Zaire

Elven culture in the post-imperial era is fragmented. Some elves live in small communities trying to preserve what remains of their traditions without the imperial baggage. Others have assimilated into the free kingdoms. The [[Zaire Pantheon]] worshipped across much of Solira is a syncretic mutation of the old elven religion. The elves have complicated feelings about this.

# Faun

## Overview

Fauns are fey-rooted peoples with a deep connection to the wild places of the [[Mortal Plane]]. Their magical aptitude grows when the [[Fey Wild]] draws near.

## Appearance


## Culture in Zaire

Faun communities tend toward the pastoral and rural, forest settlements, meadow villages, places where the boundary between the mortal world and the Fey Wild feels thin. They are generally less affected by the political upheavals of the free kingdoms than urban-dwelling peoples.

### In The Kingdom of Kavittany:

This is less true in [[Kingdom of Kavittany]] though where a strong faun [[Nobility in Solira|nobility]] exists. The [[Maximus Lovelock II|Lovelock family]] being of particular note, who have served to hand of the king for decades. Many of the most prominent Faun families live at the border of [[Sivania]] and facilitate trade between the elves of the region, the [[Katari]] of the [[Kilanji Plains]] and the more human dominated parts of west Kavittany.

This prosperity is not without drawbacks though. A powerful group of bandits operate in area called [[Pine River Clan]] which is primarily made up of fauns. They use their high mobility to reek havoc on traders and traveler in the area.

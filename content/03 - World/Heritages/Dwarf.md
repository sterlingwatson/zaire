# Dwarf

## Overview

Dwarves were the first mortals, created by [[Or]], or more accurately, by [[Kavra]] and [[Orunn]], from mud and rock. They are of the land itself, and the orbital cycles of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] do not touch them. This is a point of quiet pride. 

## Appearance

"Dwarves are most easily recognized as short humanoids with square frames, dense musculature, and thick hair. Their average height ranges from 4 to 5 ½ feet, and they are often broad in proportion to their stature. Their skin and nails contain a high amount of keratin, making them naturally resilient. This allows dwarves to embed gemstones into their bodies and decorate themselves with tattoos or piercings. Their hair grows thickly—usually on their heads, but some dwarves have thick hair across their bodies as well. Dwarves of all genders can grow facial hair, which they often style in elaborate arrangements. Typically, dwarves live up to 250 years of age, maintaining their muscle mass well into later life" - Daggerheart Core Rule Book

## Culture in Zaire

Dwarven culture is the oldest continuous civilization on [[Solira]] (which they call **Tharrok**). They keep the [[Dating Systems|Dwarven Deep Count]], worship [[Kavra]] and [[Orunn]] rather than the simplified [[Or]] of the surface pantheon, and venerate ancestor spirits called the Reckoned. The [[The Blasted Lands|Dwarven Triple Alliance]] is the primary dwarven political entity in the current era. See [[The Dwarven Faith]] for their religious tradition.

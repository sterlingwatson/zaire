# Ribbet

## Overview

Ribbets are frog-like peoples with a draconic-rooted connection to [[Elysium]]. Like other Elysium-attuned peoples, their magical capacity has been diminished by the anchoring of the Fey Wild.

## Culture in Zaire

Ribbet communities tend toward wetlands, river deltas, and coastal marshes. Their connection to water is cultural as much as biological.

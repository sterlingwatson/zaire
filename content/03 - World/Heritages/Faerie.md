# Faerie

## Overview

Faeries are among the oldest beings in the world's cosmology. They did not come from the will of living gods rather they were born from the death of [[The One]], when [[Yaaw]] shaped the [[Fey Wild]] from the divine corpse. This sets them apart from every mortal race. See [[On Faeries]] for the full picture.

## Appearance


## Culture in Zaire

Faerie culture is defined by displacement. The anchoring of the Fey Wild and the elven slave trade shattered the four courts, [[The Chrysalis Court|Chrysalis]], [[The Gilded Court|Gilded]], [[The Hollow Court|Hollow]], and [[The Thornwood Court|Thornwood]]. Faeries in the [[Mortal Plane]] are exiles, refugees, or the descendants of exiles. [[The Hooded One]]'s corruption of the Fey has made return impossible for most.

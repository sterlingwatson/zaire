# Heritages

The peoples of [[Solira]], organized by their [[The Three Realms|realm attunement]]. A people's attunement determines which orbital realm empowers them — when that realm draws near the [[Mortal Plane]], their magical aptitude grows.

---

## Fey-Rooted
*Empowered when the [[Fey Wild]] is near.*

[[Elf]] • [[Faerie]] • [[Faun]] • [[Firbolg]] • [[Gnome]]* • [[Goblin]]

## Draconic-Rooted
*Empowered when [[Elysium]] is near.*

[[Drakona]] • [[Galapa]] • [[Giant]] • [[Kobold]]* • [[Orc]] • [[Ribbet]]

## Mortal-Rooted
*Empowered when the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]] is near.*

[[Aethris]]* • [[Halfling]] • [[Human]] • [[Infernis]] • [[Katari]] • [[Simia]]

## Land-Born
*Unaffected by orbital cycles, created from the land itself.*

[[Clank]] • [[Dwarf]] • [[Fungril]]

# Elysium

---

## Overview

Elysium is one of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] created by [[Yaaw]]. It is the realm of draconic souls. In the orbital model, it is represented as red.

The peoples whose natures are rooted in Elysium: Drakona, Gallapa, Ribbets, Kobolds, Giants, Orcs, gain greater power when this realm is near the [[Mortal Plane]]. [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt was timed to a period when Elysium was distant and draconic peoples were at diminished capacity.

The destruction of dragonkind during the Great Hunt, combined with the [[Lucitans]] being forced to burn dragon bodies before their spirits could be guided, means Elysium carries an enormous weight of fragmented, unresolved draconic death. [[Sar'eth]] has been holding space for these spirits in the volcano.

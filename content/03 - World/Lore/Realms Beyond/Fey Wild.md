# The Fey Wild

> *It was meant to orbit. It was meant to draw close and pull away. That was the design. The elves decided the design was inconvenient.*

---

## Overview

The Fey Wild is one of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] created by [[Yaaw]] from the corpse of [[The One]]. It is the realm of fae souls, the homeland of faeries, goblins, gnomes, and all fey-rooted peoples. In the orbital model, it is represented as yellow.

The Fey Wild was **anchored** by the Elven Empire in IR 3127, locked at its closest orbital point to the [[Mortal Plane]], halting the natural cycle of the three realms. This gave fey-rooted peoples (and elves most of all) permanent access to their heightened magical state. The cost was the breaking of the entire orbital system and, eventually, the corruption that [[The Hooded One]] now feeds through the anchors.

The Fey Wild contains the [[Four Diminished Courts]]: the [[The Chrysalis Court|Chrysalis Court]], the [[The Gilded Court|Gilded Court]], the [[The Hollow Court|Hollow Court]], and the [[The Thornwood Court|Thornwood Court]]. [[The Hedge]] is the liminal border between the Fey Wild and the Mortal Plane.

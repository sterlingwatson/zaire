Dearest Brother,

I urge you to come back home. It has been two years since father has passed. Things are tumultous here in the Kingdom and the Duchy for that matter. [[Duke Whitehall]] seeks to install himself as King, though he does not have the support. I have been in contact with Duke Ramshorn, and have decided to back him in a bid for the throne.

Brother I could use you help. I am unsure what is to come of all this. Remember those bandits, the [[Pine River Clan]]? They have also been causing a ruckus again lately. Raiding villages and smuggling wine into Sivainia. I know you and Hael could take care of it if you would just COME HOME!

I guess it is of no use. Maybe I can appeal to your softer side if not duty. I have been recently married to a lovely woman name [[Anos Lovelock]]. You'd like her, adventurous and brave. I tell her about you often, and she would love to finally meet you. She is from [[Asulia]].

Ever since father passed I am just out of my depth. I wasn't ready. There was so much I hadn't learned... And then things got unsettled as the are now. I wish I could have my brother by my side. I love you Max.
		Your brother,
			Al

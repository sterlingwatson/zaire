*A letter written from [[Ciradyl Gaie Lovelock]] to [[Maximus Lovelock II]]* 

Max,

	I'm sorry it has taken me so long to write to you with a decision. It has been difficult for me considering my familial obligations. I have decided to come with you! Please bring the Orca in the next fortnight, I will be ready! I cannot wait to sail the seas with you, and to strike terror into the hearts of sailors everywhere.

	My father will be upset that I broke the betrothal, I'm sure he will send mercenaries after us, though as you said "nothing will be able to stop us". Don't think that I will be a mouth to feed, I will be sure to grab my raiper. If you thought I was a great dancer you haven't seen anything yet!

	I long to be in your arms again. I can hardly count the hours now that I have determined this path! Love, adventure, and the open sea! Till we are reunited.
	
	Yours forever,
		Cira

# Fungril

## Overview

Fungril, like dwarves, were created from the land itself and are unaffected by the orbital cycles of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]]. They are fungal in nature — mushroom-folk whose biology is fundamentally different from the other mortal races.

## Culture in Zaire

Fungril communities such as [[Mok Village]] tend to be insular but not hostile. Their alien biology sometimes makes integration with other cultures difficult, but those who take the time to understand Fungril ways find a people with deep knowledge of the earth and its cycles.

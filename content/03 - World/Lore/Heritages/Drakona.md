# Drakona

## Overview

The Drakona are the mortal descendants of dragonkind — the survivors of [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt, which destroyed 99% of all dragons. They carry the resonance of [[Elysium]] in their blood, and their power grows when that realm draws near the [[Mortal Plane]]. In the current era, with the [[Fey Wild]] artificially anchored and Elysium distant, the Drakona are at diminished magical capacity — a state that has persisted for over eight centuries.

## Culture in Zaire

The Drakona remember what was done to them. [[Sar'eth]] survived; most did not. Drakona communities in [[Solira]] carry a deep cultural wound from the Great Hunt, and their relationship to elves — even the scattered elves of the current era — is complicated at best. They call the continent **Vyremor**.

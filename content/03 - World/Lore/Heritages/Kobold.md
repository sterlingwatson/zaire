# Kobold

## Overview

Kobolds are small, draconic-rooted peoples connected to [[Elysium]]. Like the Drakona, Galapa, and other Elysium-attuned peoples, Kobolds have been at diminished magical capacity since the anchoring of the [[Fey Wild]] disrupted the orbital cycles. Their connection to dragonkind, while more distant than the Drakona's, is culturally significant.

## Culture in Zaire

Kobold communities in [[Solira]] tend toward the industrious and communal. Their small stature and draconic heritage place them in an interesting cultural position — connected to the legacy of dragonkind but rarely acknowledged by the Drakona as kin. Kobold attitudes toward [[Sar'eth]] and the Great Hunt vary by community.

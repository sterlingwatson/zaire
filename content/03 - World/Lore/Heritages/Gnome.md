# Gnome

## Overview

Gnomes are the mortal-realm remnant of the [[The Gilded Court|Gilded Court]] — the fae court that served as brokers for the Elven Empire's taking of fae and goblins from the [[Fey Wild]]. Their presence in the mortal realm is not innocent. Their immortality is not accidental. See [[On Gnomes]] for the full picture.

## Culture in Zaire

Gnome culture carries the weight of complicity. The Gilded Court traded their own kind to the elves in exchange for power and position. When [[The Hooded One]] took the Fey Wild, the Gilded Court was destroyed first. Many gnomes became [[Redcaps]] — corrupted remnants who must continuously shed blood to survive. Gnomes in the mortal realm live with this legacy, whether they acknowledge it or not.

# The Hedge

> *It was always thorned. It was not always a wall.*

---

## Overview

The Hedge is the liminal space between the [[Mortal Plane]] and the [[Fey Wild]], a living, thorned, semi-sentient border that was never meant to be permanent. Before the elven anchors locked the Fey Wild in place, the Hedge was a threshold: something you passed through, crossed, navigated. Now it is closer to a prison wall that grew inward.

See also: [[On Faeries]], [[The Fey Wild]], [[The Thornwood Court]], [[Anchors]]

---

## Nature

The Hedge is not simply a barrier. It is **alive** in some meaningful sense, not intelligent exactly, but responsive. It grows toward conflict. It thickens around wounds. Since the Fey Wild was locked and since [[The Hooded One]] began his corruption of the Fey, the Hedge has become grotesque: overgrown, labyrinthine, and stranger than it was.

Things live in the Hedge that live nowhere else, creatures that got caught in the crossing when the anchors were set and never made it through to either side. Neither mortal nor fae. Creatures of the threshold itself.

## Before the Locking

When the Fey Wild orbited the Mortal Plane freely, the Hedge was crossable, at the right times, in the right places, with the right knowledge. The elves discovered ways to cross it at will, which was both the beginning of their power and the beginning of the catastrophe.

## The Anchors

The elven anchors do not just lock the Fey Wild in orbit, they are embedded in the Hedge as much as in the [[Mortal Plane]]. When the anchors were set, the Hedge stopped being a threshold and became a wall. The overgrowth since then has been continuous.

[[The Hooded One]]'s corruption has infected the anchors themselves, meaning the Hedge is now also carrying his hatred outward, toward the mortal realm, toward [[Sehanine]], toward everything the anchors connect.

When the anchors are destroyed, what the Hedge becomes is an open question.

---

## The Thornwood Court

The [[Thornwood Court]] has the deepest relationship with the Hedge of any of the fae courts. It was always their borderland. Their corruption is reflected in the Hedge's current state the same way a wound on a body shows on the skin above it.

---

## Crossing the Hedge

Currently, crossing the Hedge is dangerous and disorienting. The overgrowth makes navigation unreliable. The Hedge creatures make it actively hostile. And the corruption means that anyone who spends too long inside risks carrying something back with them.

# The Three Realms

> *The Mortal Plane does not sit still in the universe. It is orbited. Three great realms circle it in slow, ancient patterns, and when they draw close, the world remembers what it is made of.*

---

## Overview

[[Yaaw]] — born from the corpse of [[The One]] — created three realms parallel to the [[Zaire]]: the **[[Fey Wild]]**, the **[[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]]**, and **[[Elysium]]**. These realms do not exist at a fixed distance from the Mortal Plane. They orbit it, in slow cycles, drawing near and pulling away across years and centuries. When a realm draws close, the peoples whose natures are rooted in it feel the change.

The three realms also serve as the destinations of souls after death, unless a particular god claims a soul to their own plane instead.

---

## The Orbital Model

Imagine the Mortal Plane at the center. Three realms circle it:

- **The Fey Wild**: yellow in the orbital model
- **The Hushed Lands**: blue in the orbital model  
- **Elysium**: red in the orbital model

Their orbits are not synchronized. At any given time, one realm may be near while another is distant. Historical events have often been shaped by which realm was dominant in a given era the height of the Elven Empire coincided with the Fey Wild being anchored at its closest point, and it is not a coincidence that elven power peaked when fae-rooted magic was at its strongest.

The **anchoring** of the Fey Wild by the Elven Empire was a deliberate freezing of this orbital cycle locking the Fey Wild at its point of maximum proximity and denying the natural rotation. This disrupted the cycles of all three realms, with consequences that are still unfolding.

---

## Magical Attunement by Ancestry

Different peoples draw magical strength from different realms. As a realm draws near, the peoples rooted in it grow more powerful. As it recedes, they diminish.

### Fey-Rooted Peoples
*Empowered when the [[Fey Wild]] is near:*
Faeries, Elves, Gnomes, Goblins, Firbolg, Faun

These peoples have the greatest magical aptitude of any mortal group when the Fey Wild is at close orbit. The elves understood this and built an empire on it. The anchoring was as much about sustaining their own power as it was about dominating the fae. As they held a monopoly on power many of the other fey-rooted species were never able to maximize the power.

### Draconic-Rooted Peoples
*Empowered when [[Elysium]] is near:*
Drakona, Gallapa, Ribbets, Kobolds, Giants, Orcs

[[Xail]]'s Great Hunt was timed, whether deliberately or instinctively, to a period when the Fey Wild's anchoring had destabilized the orbital cycle and Elysium was not at its closest. The drakona and dragons were fighting at diminished capacity. [[Sar'eth]]'s survival and eventual power may be partly explained by his absorption of so much concentrated grief and death, he carried Elysium's resonance within him when the realm itself was distant.

### Hushed Lands-Rooted Peoples
*Empowered when the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]] is near:*
Humans, Halflings, Katari, Simia, Infernis, Aethris

The Hushed Lands is the realm most associated with mortal death and the passage of ordinary souls. They are neither elevated nor diminished as dramatically as fey or draconic peoples by orbital cycles.

### Unaffected Peoples
Dwarves and Fungril were created exclusively from the land itself, from [[Orunn]]'s domain, and have no resonance with any of the three orbiting realms. The cycles do not touch them. This is part of why dwarven civilization has been among the most stable across history.

## The Three Realms as Afterlives

Each realm receives souls after death, depending on the soul's nature and connection:

**The Fey Wild** receives fae souls, or did, before the anchoring disrupted the cycle. The [[Lucitans]] were the mechanism by which fae souls reached the [[The Great Tree|Great Tree]] and returned coherently to the Fey Wild's fabric. With the anchoring, this process broke. Souls have been accumulating, fragmented and unheld, for centuries. See [[The Lucitans]].

**Elysium** receives draconic souls. The destruction of dragonkind during the Great Hunt, combined with the Lucitans being forced to burn dragon bodies in the volcano before their spirits could be guided, means Elysium is carrying an enormous weight of fragmented, unresolved draconic death. [[Sar'eth]] has been holding space for these spirits in the volcano. With his return to [[Zaire]] it is an open question what happens to the remaining drakonic people souls.

**The Hushed Lands** receives most mortal souls, the ordinary dead, the vast majority of people who have ever lived. The [[Aethris]] and their patron [[Corvina]] were responsible for guiding souls through this passage. With Corvina lost and the Aethris gone, what is happening to mortal souls now is an open question as well.

The elves, without knowing it, have completely broken the cycle of life and afterlife for all of those people. The consequence of this may be more profound than even the gods may know.

---

## The Broken Cycle

The anchoring of the Fey Wild did not simply freeze one realm in place. It introduced a rupture into a system that had been in motion since Yaaw set it running. The consequences:

- Fey-rooted peoples have been at artificially elevated power for centuries, distorting the world's magical ecology, though [[The Hedge]]'s overgrowth as prevented much of the magic from reaching [[Zaire]]
- Draconic-rooted peoples have been at diminished power for the same period.
- The Hushed Lands' orbit is somewhere stuck in the middle, affecting the passage of mortal souls
- The [[Hooded One]]'s corruption of the anchors is now feeding back through the system, accelerating the destabilization

Destroying the anchors will not simply free the Fey Wild. It will restart the orbital cycle entirely. The world will change in ways that are difficult to predict.

Not everyone will welcome this.

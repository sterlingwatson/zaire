# The 13 Major Gods of the Zaire Pantheon

> *Names and aspects may vary by region, but these are the forms known to Misty, Klix, and Talara*

The pantheon worshipped across much of [[Zaire]] in the present era is a syncretic mutation of the old elven religion. The same gods, reinterpreted through eight centuries of freedom and cultural drift. Regional variations are significant, a coastal fishing village's [[Comforce]] looks different from a mountain kingdom's, but the core thirteen are recognized everywhere. Worship or recognition of [[Yaaw]] was forbidden under the Elven Empire, as the elves held that mortality was punishment and justification for servitude, not a virtue. That prohibition has loosened in most of the free kingdoms, but old taboos run deep.

---

## [[Sehanine]]
**Domains:** Moon • Night • Kindness • Stars • Sleep • Dreaming • Doubt • Justice • Shields • Necromancy • Eye Color • Amethyst  • Purple
**Relationships:** Sister to [[Xail]], Wife to [[Asel]]
**Appearance:** An elf with flowing wavy hair, kind gaze with deep blue eyes, draped in long flowing silks. Representation of a good monarch, just and fair. Listens to her people
**Symbol:** Crescent moon surrounded by stars

## [[Asel]]
**Domains:** Sun • Fire • Passion • Reflections • Summer • Heat • Hearths • Iron • Mountain Tops • Volcanos • Ruby • Red
**Relationships:** Wife to [[Sehanine]]
**Appearance:** Human female with deep mahogany skin, short curly hair, brown-red eyes, muscular build. Depicted in plate mail or sensible tunic. Representation of a strong ruler at war. Decisive and fearsome, while also being deliberate. 
**Symbol:** Blazing sun over mountain peaks

---

## [[Storse]]
**Domains:** Luck • Commerce • Trade • Wealth • Gold • Merchants • Greed • Diamond 
**Ancestry:** Faun
**Appearance:** Billy goat features, rounded belly, often playing lute or guitar. Not particularly strong in build
**Personality:** Appears kind and jovial, but cutthroat and conniving in business dealings.
**Symbol:** Bag of gold coins with a lute

---

## [[Or]]
**Domains:** Earth • Craftspeople • The Forge • Brewing • Fermentation • Platonic Love • Mead • Rocks • Jet • Black
**Ancestry:** Dwarf *(represents qualities elves attribute to dwarves)*
**Unique Aspect:** Portrayed as both **Moridin** (father god) and **Hallel** (mother god) from the [[Dwarven Pantheon]]. In either case they are always bearded with long brown hair and muscular. They are either clad in perfect plate mail or in sensible dwarven robes.
**Personality:** Revels in friendship, drink, and hard work. Slow to befriend but solid as a rock. 
**Symbol:** Hammer and a tankard of mead

---

## [[Lorta]]
**Domains:** Agriculture • Orchards • Fruit • Flowers • Bees • Spring • Honey • Parks • Composting • Pumpkins • Topaz • Orange
**Ancestry:** Firbolg
**Personality:** Sweet and softspoken. A hard worker who love the outdoors and insects.  Dressed in typical farming attire for the region they are in, often times including a large straw hat adorn with flowers. Lorta is not gendered typically.
**Symbol:** A single honey bee on a flower with 13 petals

---

## [[Malort]]
**Domains:** Music • Romantic Love • Language • Poetry • Painting • Songbirds • Plums • Ducks • Aquamarine • Teal
**Ancestry:** Simia
**Personality:** Joyous to the point of annoyance in some regards. Known to be always smiling and singing. Often clothed in garish attire he is the patron god of bards naturally. Almost always has a long curling mustache.
**Symbol:** A male a female duck on a ponds surface.

---

## [[Rethel]] 
**Domains:** The Coasts • Rain  • Cooks • Salt • Shellfish  • Mud • Algae • Lilypads • Lotus Flower • Amphibians • Metamorphosis • Sand • Floods • Pearl • Grey
**Ancestry:** Ribbet
**Personality:** Quiet and serious but with a keen since of hospitality. Known to feed you the most excellent dish. Usually depicted with an apron on.
**Symbol:** A blue crab with a rain cloud above.

---

## [[Seedlip]] 
**Domains:** Knowledge • Children • Childhood • Sourness • Teachers • Play • Imagination • Wonder • The Sublime • Hallucination • Trickery • Citrine • Yellow
**Ancestry:** Halfling
**Symbol:** An open book

---

## [[Ennem]] 
**Domains:** Motherhood • Livestock • Weaving • Textiles • Painting • Blood • Stiches • Doctors/Nurses • Garnet • Oxblood/Burgundy
**Ancestry:** Giant - Motherly in middle age. Here eyes run the gamut, but most often two. She is dressed in beautifully woven fabrics in which ever style the depiction is being made. Vampires tend to worship Ennem as her dominion over blood sees them not as monsters but as creatures of her love.
**Symbol:** A sheep in the bottom half and a spinning loom in the top half of a circular crest

---

## [[Xail]]
**Domains:** The Hunt • Wilderness • Rivers • Shade • Foraging • Mushrooms • Autumn • Archery • Bone • Snakes • Necromancy (shared with Sehanine)• Decay • Brown • Emerald 
**Relationships:** Sister to [[Sehanine]]
**Appearance:** Slender elf with brown hair, often depicted in forest settings with a long or recurved bow. She always has some pet though its precise species is different depending on region. In the forests of [[Sivania]] it is a brown bear, yet in the far reached of [[Kingdom of Gormly]] we find great hawks. 
**Symbol:** Three oak leaves depicted in free fall.

---

## [[Comforce]]
**Domains:** Wind • Sea • Ships • Sailors • Fatherhood • Winter • Fishermen • Timber • Storms • Lightning • Drowning • Sharks • Whales • Physical Strength •  Snow • Blue •  Sapphire
**Race:** Orc - with blue-grey skin and tusks in both directions. Armored in light leather armor designed for sailing. Known for the enjoying brotherhood of sailors and the open sea. Fearsome in battle, in legend having defeated the last Kraken. Fights with both a sword and buckler.
**Symbol:** Ship's sail billowing in in the wind

## [[Amaric]] 
**Domains:** War • Steel • Bronze •  Death in Battle • Glory • Martial Arts • Infection • Furs • Altruism • Loudness • Tigerseye • Beige 
**Race:** Katari - Usually depicted as the largest Katari variety in the local region. In much of Zaire she is female and a lioness. Though we also see her portrayed as a snow leopard in the Meyshelle Mountains and a mountain lion in the Commonwealth. She is usually fully armored in steel plate and wielding a two handed weapon that is surrounded by flame. It was Amaric who lead the gods in battle agains the Old Ones and freed the Mortal Plane from their rule.
**Symbol:** Flames cast in bronze and steel

---

## [[Yaaw]] 
**Domains:** Magic • The Underworld • Mortality • Funerals • Births • Disease • The Unknown • The Unknowable • Stars • Darkness • Silence • Reptiles • Opal • White
**Race:** Galapa - Depicted as an old or baby tortoise depending on context. Yaaw is a mysterious diety. Worshiped for being the giver and taker. In the old elven pantheon Yaaw created Farie, Shadehiem, and the Mortal Plane and with it all mortals, undead and faries. 
**Symbol:** Spiral representing the cycle of life and death

## Pantheon Relationships

### Divine Marriages
- [[Sehanine]]  ⚭ [[Asel]]
- [[Amaric]] ⚭ [[Comforce]]

### Sibling Bonds
- [[Sehanine]] ↔ [[Xail]]
- [[Rethel]] ↔ [[Yaaw]]

### Complementary Domains
- **Life Cycles:** [[Yaaw]] (births/deaths) ↔ [[Seedlip]] (childhood) ↔ [[Ennem]] (motherhood) ↔ [[Comforce]] (fatherhood)
- **Seasons:**  [[Asel]] (summer) ↔ [[Xail]] (autumn) ↔ [[Lorta]] (spring) ↔ [[Comforce]] (winter)
- **Elements:** [[Asel]] (fire) ↔ [[Or]] (earth) ↔ [[Comforce]] (air) ↔ [[Rethel]] (water)

*Note: These names and depictions represent the Southwestern Zaire regional interpretation. Other regions may know these deities by different names or emphasize different aspects of their domains.*

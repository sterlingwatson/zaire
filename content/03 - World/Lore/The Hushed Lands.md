# The Hushed Lands

---

## Overview

The Hushed Lands is one of the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] created by [[Yaaw]]. It is the realm of mortal souls — the destination of the ordinary dead, the vast majority of people who have ever lived. In the orbital model, it is represented as blue.

The peoples whose natures are rooted in the Hushed Lands: Humans, Halflings, Katari, Simia, Infernis all gain greater magical aptitude when this realm is near the [[Zaire]].

[[The Aethris]] and their patron [[Corvina]] were responsible for guiding souls through the passage into and through the Hushed Lands. With Corvina lost and the Aethris gone, mortal souls are no longer being guided. What is happening to them is an open and troubling question that no one in the main campaign has fully reckoned with yet.

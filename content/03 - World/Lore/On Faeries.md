# On Faeries

> 

---

## Overview

Faeries are among the oldest beings in the [[Mortal Plane]]'s cosmology, and among the least understood. They did not come from the will of living gods, but from the death of [[The One]]. When [[Yaaw]] shaped the three parallel realms from the divine corpse, the [[Fey Wild]] was born, and with it: faeries.

This origin sets them apart from every mortal race. Humans, elves, orcs are all are children of gods who chose to make them.

---

## Nature

The domain of [[Rethel]] — **Metamorphosis** — has a particular kinship with faerie nature, though they have never known it. Faeries lost the phase of their life in which they go through metamorphosis when they were trapped on the other side of [[The Hedge]]. It usually involves two stages. The first metamorphosis all faeries used to go through in which they became magical beings, the second metamorphosis only comes to the strongest, which allows them to move into the hedge at will and operate in the mortal plane.

---

## The Four Diminished Courts

Faerie society was once organized into courts. The Elven Empire's systematic taking of fae from the Fey Wild fractured these courts almost beyond recognition. What remains are four diminished things:

| Court               | People        | Status                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| [[Chrysalis Court]] | Insectoid fae | Scattered across the mortal realm             |
| [[Hollow Court]]    | Goblins       | Fragmented, parts in both the mortal and fey. |
| [[Thornwood Court]] | Ancient fae   | Deeply corrupted, nearly feral                |
| [[Gilded Court]]    | Gnomes        | Mostly corrupted into [[On Redcaps]]             |

---

## Faeries in the Mortal Realm

Faeries exiled to the mortal realm act as mortals do, but shades of their true selves remain in the Fey Wild. As they die in the mortal plane their shade, immortal as it is, stalks the Fey Wild, hungry and lost.

When [[Talara]] records the stories of these people in the mortal realm a connection is remade. With enough connections and through her quest to destroy the anchors she brings each being closer to its true self, its true name, and the truth itself for the fairies.

---

## The Hedge

Faeries cross between the mortal realm and the Fey Wild through [[The Hedge]]; a living, thorned, semi-sentient liminal space. Since the Fey Wild was locked in place by the elven anchors, the Hedge has become overgrown. It is harder to cross than it once was, and stranger things now live in it, but with each anchor destroyed a small path opens up.

---

## Faeries and the Gods

[[Sehanine]] has long claimed a particular relationship with the fae. The moon governs fairy rings, thresholds, transformation. But Sehanine also benefited from the system that enslaved and exiled the fae. Her relationship to faerie-kind is therefore **compromised**, however sincere her care may be.

[[Yaaw]] created the fae and holds their fate in a more fundamental sense; as god of births, deaths, magic, and the unknowable.

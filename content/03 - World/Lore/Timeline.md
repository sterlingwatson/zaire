# Timeline

> *The elves dated everything from the founding of their first city. The rest of the world dates everything from the Campaign of Broken Chains. This disagreement is itself historical.*

*All dates before the fall of the empire use [[Dating Systems|Imperial Reckoning (IR)]]. All dates after use [[Dating Systems|Free Reckoning (FR)]].*

---

## Pre-History

**The Anchoring of the Fey Wild (Year 3127)**
The elves devise a means to lock the [[Fey Wild]] at its closest orbital point to the [[Mortal Plane]], halting the natural cycle of the [[The Three Realms|three realms]]. This allows fey-rooted peoples — and elves most of all — to maintain their magically elevated state indefinitely rather than cycling with the orbits.

The cost is not yet apparent. The [[Lucitans]] are brought to the volcano. The systematic taking of fae and goblins from the Fey Wild accelerates, now that the elves have permanent access.

The [[Gilded Court]] alliance is formalized around this period.

---

## The Great Hunt (3128–3136)

[[Xail]] leads the elven campaign of extermination against dragonkind and the drakona. The timing is deliberate or instinctive — [[Elysium]] is not at its closest orbital point, and draconic peoples are at diminished power.

**3128:** The Hunt begins. Dragon and drakona cities come under coordinated attack.

**3128–3136:** Bodies are brought to the volcano. The [[Lucitans]] — already stripped of language and community, already hollowed — are made to burn dragon and drakona remains. Drakona artifacts, texts, and cultural objects are destroyed. Spirits fragment into the volcano without guidance.

**3136:** The Hunt winds down. Ninety-nine percent of all dragons are dead. The drakona are leaderless, and by extension, [[Elysium]] as a living religious tradition is nearly severed from the mortal plane. [[Sar'eth]] survives, having absorbed the grief and power of a civilization's erasure.

The Lucitans' assignment shifts from graveyard keeping to sacrifice cult. The volcano still requires feeding. [[Sar'eth]] is below.

---

## The Campaign of Broken Chains (3131–3137)

**3131:** In a village far to the north, in a ritual overnight, [[Yaaw]] gifts [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]] great magical power. The slave revolt begins. The [[The Legendary Heroes|Legendary Heroes]] begin to find each other.

Note: The Campaign begins while the Great Hunt is still ongoing. These are not sequential events. The elves are conducting a genocide and suppressing a revolt simultaneously, which is part of what makes 3134 so significant.

**3134:** The orbital disruption caused by the anchoring produces its first irreversible consequence — **the elves begin to die.** Old age arrives. Illness arrives. Their magical ability remains heightened but becomes unpredictable. The immortality that justified their empire and their theology begins to visibly fail.

This is the crack in the foundation. The empire cannot survive the death of elven exceptionalism, and elven exceptionalism cannot survive elves dying of old age in the street.

**3137:** The elves, under pressure from the revolt and with their own civilization destabilizing, **sue for peace with Drakonia**, attempting to address the external threat so they can suppress the internal one. This is the entry point of the Legendary Heroes bonus campaign.

---

## The End of the Elven Empire (IR 3137–3139)

The revolt succeeds. The Elven Empire collapses at **IR 3139** — the year that becomes **FR 1** in the free kingdoms' reckoning. The details of its final years are the content of the Legendary Heroes campaign.

[[Sar'eth]] destroys the centers of imperial power. The Legendary Heroes do not stop him. Some of them may have coordinated with him, or may have simply recognized that his targets were the same as theirs.

Bartholomew and Sar'eth make their deal. They seal themselves in the volcano. The public story — that Bartholomew locked the dragon away — begins immediately, because the truth is too complicated and the world needs a simpler story in order to rebuild.

**IR 3272 / FR 134:** The [[Jade Empire]] is founded in the east when the first Jade Emperor unites the eastern kingdoms against an Infernis invasion from the north. The eastern half of [[Solira]] begins counting its own calendar from this event.

---

## The Long Silence (IR 3139 / FR 1 – FR ~800)

Eight hundred years pass.

The world rebuilds without its founding heroes. The scale of the rebuilding is difficult to overstate — the Elven Empire's collapse left the continent in a state comparable to early medieval Europe, roughly 700s AD. Population growth was slow. Cities had to be reinvented rather than inherited. Serfdom was codified in many regions as the new kingdoms struggled to organize labor without the empire's slave infrastructure.

Elves themselves became rare. They had ruled much of the continent of [[Solira]] for millennia, but plague, aging, and the collapse of their political structures scattered them. In the present day, encountering an elf is notable.

The pantheon of gods worshipped in the kingdoms where [[Misty]] and [[Klix]] are from are syncretic mutations of the old elven religion, the same gods, reinterpreted through the lens of the peoples who overthrew their worshippers. In parts of Solira, the [[The Legendary Heroes|Legendary Heroes]] themselves are regarded as deities, though the ancestries attributed to these heroes shift to fit different communities.

The [[The Cult of the Radiant Sun|Cult of the Radiant Sun]] grows, slowly and then quickly, occupying [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]]'s legacy. The [[Fey Wild]] remains anchored. The [[Lucitans]] continue their inverted ritual above the volcano, generation after generation, with no memory of what they once were. The unguided dead accumulate.

[[Corvina]] is lost. The [[The Aethris|Aethris]] are gone. Whatever Corvina was is reincorporated into [[Yaaw]], who says nothing about it.

The two elven gods who remain, [[Sehanine]] and [[Xail]] continue. Sehanine has been siphoning fae power through the Hedge since the anchoring. The [[The Hooded One|Hooded One]]'s corruption begins threading through the anchors.

**~FR 750:** Regular trade routes between [[Pearl's Mouth]] and [[Kempeir]] are established. [[Kempeir]] transitions from provincial to cosmopolitan within a few decades. The dawn of a new age,[[Klix]] has been privy to significant changes in his lifetime.

[[Sar'eth]] leaves the volcano. The Lucitan cult is broken.

[[Talara]] begins her journey.

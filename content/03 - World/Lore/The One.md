# The One

> *They set in motion all time, the Universe, but put nothing in it.*

---

## Overview

The One is the primordial creator deity in Zaire's cosmology, the being who existed before everything else and from whose body the world was made. The One is not worshipped. They are not prayed to. They are, in fact dead.

The One created a garden from chaos and set order to it. The universe existed like that for millennia until, while sleeping, a dream produced the moon, [[Sehanine]]. Fascinated by this unconscious creation, The One was mystified. Sehanine, lonely, coaxed The One to sleep again and manipulated their dreams to produce [[Asel]], the sun, from The One's heart.

Growing tired of Sehanine and Asel's love, feeling tricked, The One tore a chunk of flesh from their own liver and molded it into the [[Zaire]], the planet itself, and placed it between the Sun and Moon to separate them. In their separation, Sehanine wept, creating the stars.

From their body, The One produced the raw materials of the world: from their lung, the seas and wind (giving life to [[Comforce]]); from their stomach, the plants (giving life to [[Lorta]]); from their spleen, vitality itself (giving birth to [[Ennem]]).

When The One finally died, [[Yaaw]] was born from the corpse, and from that death came the [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]] and the faeries.

## Notes

*The One's death as the origin of both Yaaw and faerie-kind is theologically significant — it means the fae are children of divine death, not divine will.*

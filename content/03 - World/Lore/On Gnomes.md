# On Gnomes

> *They have had thousands of years to get their story straight. Some of them still haven't managed it.*

---

## Overview

Gnomes are the mortal-realm remnant of the [[Gilded Court]] — the fae court that served as brokers for the Elven Empire's taking of fae and goblins from the [[Fey Wild]]. Their presence in the mortal realm is not innocent. Their immortality is not accidental.

See also: [[The Gilded Court]], [[On Redcaps]], [[On Faeries]]

---

## Immortality

The gnome community in the mortal realm is **immortal.** The individuals who made the decisions that enabled the elven slave trade are still alive. This is unusual — most mortal races age and die, allowing historical guilt to soften into inherited shame across generations. For gnomes, there is no such softening. The people who drew the map are still here.

The source of this immortality is unclear but likely tied to [[Storse]], the god of commerce, or to the terms of the Gilded Court's original arrangement with the Elven Empire. Immortality as payment. A deal that cannot be undone.

## The Weight of Memory

Living with perfect memory of what you did — for the entire span of mortal history since the fall of the Elven Empire — has affected different gnomes differently.

Some are **wracked with guilt**, living diminished lives in penance for something they cannot fully make right.

Some have **rationalized**. Thousands of years is a long time to perfect a justification. These gnomes have built entire theologies of survival around their choices. They do not believe they did wrong. They believe they had no choice, and they will explain why in great and practiced detail. Meeting one of these gnomes is the harder scene.

Most are somewhere in between — knowing what they did, unable to fully face it, unable to fully escape it.

## The Community

One community in the mortal realm. Insular, self-sufficient, regarded with **suspicion by other fae** (those who know their history) and with varying degrees of ignorance by mortal races who don't know what gnomes are or where they came from.

## Uncorrupted Gnomes in the Fey Wild

A small enclave of gnomes remains in the [[Fey Wild]], hidden deep in what has not yet been fully corrupted by [[The Hooded One]]. These are not heroes — they are the ones who fled when the court broke, who hid while their kin became [[On Redcaps]]. They carry the full history and they know it. They are survivors who watched from cover.

## The Question Talara Has to Answer

Does [[Talara]] record their story?

The Chronicler's power implies that recording a fae's true story restores something to them. But what is restored to the gnomes if their story is told truly? Not dignity — they have that, if uncomfortably. Not identity — they know exactly who they are. Perhaps only **accountability.** Is that enough? Is that Talara's role to deliver?

And critically: does [[Sehanine]] want her to? The goddess of justice has every reason to condemn the gnomes publicly. She also has every reason to want that condemnation to serve as a distraction from her own role in the system that used them.

# The Dwarven Faith

> *The surface folk took Orunn's name, shortened it, and forgot Kavra entirely. 

---

## Overview

The dwarven religion is among the oldest on [[Zaire]] and is, by the dwarves' own accounting, the *original* faith. It predates the elven theology by millennia. Where the [[Zaire Pantheon]] recognizes a single god called [[Or]] with domains over crafting, brewing, earth, and friendship, the dwarves know this to be a simplification. A flattening.

The dwarves worship two gods: **[[Kavra]]**, the Mother of the Forge, and **[[Orunn]]**, the Father of Stone. They are married partners and are seen as equals. One shapes; the other endures. Between them, they account for everything the surface religions compress into "Or."

Below the two gods sits a deep tradition of **ancestor veneration**, the Reckoned, great dwarves whose deeds in life earned them a place in the liturgy. The Reckoned are not gods. They are witnesses, patrons, and exemplars, invoked by name in prayer and or during craftdwaftship.

---

## The Two Gods

### [[Kavra]]: The Mother of the Forge
**Domains:** Creation • Shaping • The Forge • Crafting • Gems • Innovation • Precision
**Titles:** The First Hammer, She Who Shapes, The Gemcutter

Kavra is the active principle. She takes raw material and makes it into something. Every act of creation, from forging a blade to cutting a gem to designing an aqueduct, is Kavra's work. She represents the moment when potential becomes actual. 

Her worship is the workshop. Dwarven smiths, jewelers, masons, and engineers pray to Kavra not in temples but at their anvils, workbenches, and drafting tables. The act of making and designing is the prayer.

### [[Orunn]] — The Father of Stone
**Domains:** Brewing • Endurance • Stone • Earth • Metals • Martial Prowess • Patience • The Deep
**Titles:** The Unyielding, He Who Holds, The Brewer

Orunn is the enduring principle. He is the mountain unmoving. Every act of persistence from standing a watch, to holding a line, or waiting for the right moment is Orunn's domain.

Brewing is Orunn's most sacred craft. It requires patience, precision in timing rather than shaping, and the willingness to let time do work. A dwarven brewmaster is as much a priest of Orunn as any cleric.

Martial prowess falls under Orunn not because the dwarves are warlike, but because their approach to war is about endurance. About outlasting, holding ground, wearing down. Dwarven warfare is theology made tactical.

---

## The Marriage

Kavra and Orunn are not opposed. They are not in tension. They are partners one cannot exist without the other. Kavra shapes what Orunn provides. Orunn preserves what Kavra creates. A sword is Kavra's art and Orunn's metal. A hold is Kavra's engineering and Orunn's stone. A feast is Kavra's cooking and Orunn's brewing.

The dwarves do not understand how the surface races could look at this and see one god. It would be like looking at a marriage and seeing only one spouse. The theological flattening of Kavra and Orunn into "Or" is, to dwarven sensibilities, not just wrong, it is *rude*. It erases Kavra entirely. The elves took the father's name, kept his domains of earth and stone, grafted on some of Kavra's crafting domains as though they were afterthoughts, and never mentioned the mother at all. This is a quiet wound atop all the other suffered by the dwarves.

---

## The Reckoned, Ancestor Spirits

Below Kavra and Orunn, the dwarven faith venerates the **Reckoned**: ancestors whose deeds were great enough to earn them a permanent place in the liturgy. The Reckoned are not gods. They do not grant divine power. They are exemplars. Their lives are studied, their decisions debated, their names invoked as witnesses when oaths are sworn or work begun.

A dwarf beginning a difficult forge project might say *"Bera watch my hammer"*, invoking a Reckoned smith. A brewer starting a new batch might invoke a Reckoned brewmaster. A soldier holding a wall might call on a Reckoned warrior. It is not prayer rather a simple acknowledgment. *You did this well. I will try to do it well too.*

### Notable Reckoned

**Bera Ironvow**: The Smith. Legendarily created the first mithral alloy that could hold an edge through a century of use. Invoked by smiths and engineers to design something that would retain its usefulness long beyond its time.

**Tholmir the Patient**: The Brewmaster. Said to have begun a brew that took three generations to finish, with instructions left for his apprentice's apprentice. Invoked by brewers, and by anyone undertaking work they know they will not live to complete. 

**Grata Shieldborne**: The Defender. Held a passage in the deep against an incursion for eleven days without relief. Invoked by soldiers, guards, mothers, and anyone who must endure.

**Durik Gemtongue**: The Diplomat. Negotiated the founding of the [[The Blasted Lands|Dwarven Triple Alliance]], uniting three holds that had been feuding for years after the expulsion of the dwarves from the [[Dwarven Empire]]. Invoked by diplomats, merchants, and anyone who must find common ground.

**Maetha Deepdelve**: The Explorer. Mapped tunnels that connected the three holds of the Triple Alliance. Invoked by miners, explorers, and anyone venturing into the unknown.

*Additional Reckoned emerge from play and from dwarven NPCs who mention their personal patron ancestors.*

## Worship in Practice

Dwarven worship does not look like surface worship. There are no sermons. The faith is embedded in the rhythms of dwarven life:

**At the forge**, a prayer to Kavra before the first strike, spoken or silent. The quality of the work *is* the offering.

**At the cask**, a prayer to Orunn when a new brew is sealed. The patience to wait *is* the devotion.

**At oaths**, the Reckoned are named as witnesses. Breaking an oath sworn before a named ancestor is among the gravest offenses in dwarven culture.

**At funerals**, the community decides whether the dead have earned the right to be Reckoned. Most are not. This is not a slight rather it is an acknowledgment that the Reckoned are exceptional, and that being a good dwarf who lived well is its own honor.

**At the Deep Count**, the [[Dating Systems|Dwarven Deep Count]] is itself an act of faith. The dwarves have been keeping time since before anyone else. The unbroken record is Orunn's endurance made manifest.

## The Dwarven View of the Zaire Pantheon

The dwarves do not deny the existence of the other gods. [[Sehanine]], [[Asel]], [[Yaaw]] etc. these are real. The dwarves simply note that they are *other people's gods*. Kavra and Orunn came first. The [[Zaire]] was shaped from [[The One]]'s flesh by Orunn's endurance and Kavra's hands. The dwarves were the first mortals, made from the land itself.

The syncretic mutation that turned Kavra and Orunn into "[[Or]]" is understood as a consequence of the elven empire's theological apparatus, the elves categorized everything into their own framework, and the subject peoples inherited that framework after liberation. The free kingdoms worship "Or" because that's what they were taught.

Dwarves and Fungril, being created from the land, are unaffected by the [[The Three Realms|orbital cycles]] of the Fey Wild, Hushed Lands, and Elysium. This is a point of quiet pride. The world wobbles. The dwarves do not.

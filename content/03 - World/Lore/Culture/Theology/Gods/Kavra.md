# Kavra

**Domains:** Creation • Shaping • The Forge • Crafting • Gems • Innovation • Precision
**Titles:** The First Hammer, She Who Shapes, The Gemcutter
**Relationships:** Wife to [[Orunn]], one half of what the surface religions call [[Or]]
**Appearance:** A broad-shouldered dwarven woman with hands blackened by forge soot, eyes that reflect firelight even in darkness. Depicted holding a hammer in one hand and an uncut gem in the other. Her hair is braided with wire.
**Symbol:** A hammer crossed with a chisel over an uncut gem

---

## Overview

Kavra is the Mother of the Forge — the active, creative half of the dwarven dual pantheon. Where [[Orunn]] endures, Kavra *acts*. She is the moment the hammer falls, the cut that reveals the gem's facets, the design that turns raw stone into a hold that will stand for millennia.

In the [[Zaire Pantheon]], Kavra does not exist. Her domains — crafting, forge work, gems — were absorbed into the surface god [[Or]], who is in truth a flattened version of Orunn with Kavra's attributes bolted on as afterthoughts. The erasure of Kavra from the surface religions is a quiet grievance among the dwarves. They lost the mother.

See also: [[The Dwarven Faith]], [[Orunn]], [[Or]]

---

## Worship

Kavra is worshipped at the anvil, the jeweler's bench, the mason's chisel, the engineer's drafting table. The act of making something well is itself a prayer. A smith who produces shoddy work has not just failed their craft — they have failed Kavra.

Dwarven children learning a trade are taught that Kavra watches the first strike. If you begin well, the work follows. If you begin carelessly, no amount of correction will fully fix it.

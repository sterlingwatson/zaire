# Or

**Domains:** Crafting • Brewing • Earth • Dwarves • Forge Work • Friendship
**Relationships:** Created from [[The One]]'s body
**Appearance:** A stout, gruff figure. Depicted differently by region — sometimes as a dwarf, sometimes as a stocky human, always with calloused hands and practical clothes. Never depicted as elegant or refined.
**Symbol:** Hammer and tankard

---

## Overview

Or is the god of earth, crafting, and brewing in the [[Zaire Pantheon]] — a stout and gruff deity who shaped the [[Mortal Plane]] from a chunk of [[The One]]'s flesh and created the Dwarves from mud and rock to keep company in the barren lands. The twelfth month of the [[Zaire Calendar]], **Brewcraft**, is dedicated to Or.

In the free kingdoms, Or is worshipped as a single god encompassing crafting, forge work, brewing, earth, and the bonds of friendship. Craft fairs, brewing competitions, and friendship festivals mark the month of Brewcraft.

---

## The Dwarven Correction

The dwarves would like the surface races to know that **Or is not one god.**

What the [[Zaire Pantheon]] calls "Or" is a flattened, simplified version of two dwarven deities: **[[Kavra]]**, the Mother of the Forge (creation, shaping, gems, crafting, precision), and **[[Orunn]]**, the Father of Stone (endurance, brewing, stone, earth, metals, martial prowess). They are married partners and equals.

The name "Or" is a mangling of "Orunn" — the elves took the father's name, shortened it, grafted on some of Kavra's domains, and erased the mother entirely. The syncretic religions that emerged after the fall of the Elven Empire inherited this simplification without ever questioning it.

The dwarves consider this deeply disrespectful, though most have stopped bringing it up. See [[The Dwarven Faith]] for the full dwarven theological tradition.

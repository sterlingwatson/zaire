# Orunn

**Domains:** Brewing • Endurance • Stone • Earth • Metals • Martial Prowess • Patience • The Deep
**Titles:** The Unyielding, He Who Holds, The Brewer of Ages
**Relationships:** Husband to [[Kavra]], the god the surface religions reduced to [[Or]]
**Appearance:** A massive dwarven man carved from living rock, with veins of ore visible beneath granite skin. His beard is long enough to pool at his feet. He is always depicted seated or standing, never rushing, never straining. Depicted holding a tankard or a shield, never both.
**Symbol:** A tankard set atop a mountain peak

---

## Overview

Orunn is the Father of Stone, the enduring, patient half of the dwarven dual pantheon. Where [[Kavra]] shapes, Orunn waits. He is the stone that waits to be found, the ore that forms over ages, the patience to let the brew age until it is ready and not a day before.

The surface god [[Or]] is derived from Orunn, his name mangled, his wife erased, his domains mixed with Kavra's until the distinction was lost. Of the two gods, the surface races kept a shadow of Orunn and forgot Kavra entirely. The dwarves note that this is typical. Everyone remembers the stone. No one remembers who shaped it.

See also: [[The Dwarven Faith]], [[Kavra]], [[Or]]

## Worship

Orunn is worshipped through patience. Brewing is his most sacred craft, not because the dwarves love drink (though they do) but because brewing requires the willingness to let time do the work. A brewer who rushes fermentation has not just failed the recipe. They have failed Orunn.

Martial worship of Orunn is not about aggression. Dwarven soldiers pray to Orunn before a battle not for strength to attack, but for endurance to hold. The dwarven approach to warfare is defensive, positional, grinding, Orunn's theology expressed in tactics. You do not break through a dwarven line. You exhaust yourself against it.

The [[Dating Systems|Dwarven Deep Count]] is Orunn's endurance made liturgical. The unbroken count stretching back over five thousand years is itself an act of worship proof that the dwarves can hold to something longer than anyone else, and that the record will endure.

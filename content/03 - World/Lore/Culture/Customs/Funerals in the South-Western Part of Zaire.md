---
title: Funerals in the South-Western Part of Zaire
tags:
  - lore
type: lore
---

In much of [[Zaire]] [[Yaaw]] is the patron god of death, and it is his priests that help to guide the souls on to the afterlife in the person respective place.
Funerals are led by a local priest. First as many white tapestries are found as possible and hung to flow in the wind. 
The body is wrapped in white cloth and placed on a pyre to be burned. It is often done in near silence. The priest may say a few words, close family of friends may also. But eventually the pyre is lit and as the flames absorb the body the weight of the sound of the flames is left to linger.
Each ancestry often has a tradition for the parts of the body that are left. Fauns will often take the horns of the deceased. Galapa will take the shell. Human will stack the remaining bone fragments in an urn.

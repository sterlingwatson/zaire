# Dating Systems

> *The elves dated everything from the founding of their first city. The dwarves have been counting since before anyone else started. The Jade Empire counts from a demon invasion. The free kingdoms count from the day the chains broke. 

---

## The Four Calendars

Zaire uses at least four distinct dating systems across its major civilizations. Which calendar a text, monument, or speaker uses tells you as much about their politics and geography as it does about the date.

---

### Imperial Reckoning (IR)

The Elven Empire counted years from the founding of its first city-state. All dates from the imperial era; treaties, censuses, military campaigns, scholarly works all use this system. The dates in the 3000s that appear in older texts and in the [[Timeline]] are Imperial Reckoning dates.

The empire's own records end around IR 3139. Elven ruins, inscriptions, and surviving texts all use IR dating. Scholars who study the imperial period still reference IR dates for precision.

**Key IR dates:**
- **IR 3127**: The [[The Pyramid of First Anchoring|Anchoring]] of the [[Fey Wild]]
- **IR 3128–3136**: [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt against dragonkind
- **IR 3131**: Beginning of the [[The Legendary Heroes|Campaign of Broken Chains]]
- **IR 3134**: The elves begin to age and sicken
- **IR 3137**: The elves sue for peace with Drakonia; the Legendary Heroes campaign begins
- **IR 3139**: Fall of the Elven Empire. Year 1 of the Free Reckoning.

---

### Free Reckoning (FR)

After the fall of the Elven Empire, the free peoples of western [[Solira]] adopted a new calendar counting from the empire's collapse. **FR 1 = IR 3139**.

All dates in the main campaign use Free Reckoning. The current campaign takes place in **FR 812–813**.

**Key FR dates:**
- **FR 812–813**: The current campaign year

---

### Jade Empire Dynastic Calendar (JE)

The [[Jade Empire]] in eastern [[Solira]] keeps its own calendar, counting from the first dynasty established after the Infernis invasion from the north. **JE 1 = IR 3272** — the year the first Jade Emperor united the eastern kingdoms against the demonic incursion and founded a dynasty.

The Jade Empire's calendar predates the Free Reckoning by over a century. Its use signals allegiance to the eastern imperial tradition and its vassal states. The [[Memory Stone]] and other artifacts of Infernis artificers are dated in JE years.

In the current campaign year: **FR 812 = JE 679**.

---

### Dwarven Deep Count (DC)

The dwarves have been keeping time longer than anyone. Their calendar predates the Elven Empire by nearly two millennia, at the fall of the empire (IR 3139 / FR 1), the Dwarven Deep Count was already at **DC 5051**.

The Deep Count is measured from an event in dwarven pre-history that non-dwarven scholars have never been given a clear explanation for. [[Orunn]]'s children do not volunteer this information. The dwarves, created from the land itself and unaffected by the [[The Three Realms|orbital cycles]], have maintained an unbroken record longer than any other civilization on [[Solira]].

In the current campaign year: **FR 812 = DC 5862**.

## Cultural Significance

**Imperial Reckoning**: Using IR dates in casual speech is not taboo, but it marks the speaker. Scholars use IR for the imperial period, goblin scholars are careful to use all three in their studies. The [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]] uses IR selectively when it suits their narrative. Most people in the free kingdoms have never heard an IR date and would not know what to do with one.

**Free Reckoning**: The standard across the western free kingdoms. For most people, the world begins with [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]] and the freedom that followed. Everything before that is simply *before*.

**Jade Empire Dynastic**: Used throughout the eastern half of Solira. The Jade Empire and its vassal states view their calendar as a mark of civilization distinct from both the elven past and the western liberation narrative. To use JE dating in the west is to signal eastern education or trade connections.

**Dwarven Deep Count**: Rarely used outside dwarven communities and the [[The Blasted Lands|Dwarven Triple Alliance]]. When a dwarf tells you the year, the number is always unsettlingly large.

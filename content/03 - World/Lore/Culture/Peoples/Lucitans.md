# The Lucitans

---

## Overview

The Lucitans were once the mechanism by which fae souls reached the Great Tree and returned coherently to the [[Fey Wild]]'s fabric. They were brought to the volcano by the Elven Empire during the [[The Pyramid of First Anchoring|Anchoring]] (IR 3127) and systematically stripped of language, community, and identity.

During the Great Hunt (IR 3128–3136), the Lucitans were forced to burn dragon and drakona remains in the volcano, preventing those spirits from being guided to [[Elysium]] and fragmenting them. When [[Sar'eth]] is sealed a deal is made with the Lucitans to maintain a shrine there and allow for the drakona souls to be safe until Elysium could return close to orbit and they could return. [[Sar'eth]] and [[Bartholomew the Chainbreaker|Bartholomew]] did not know that it was the anchors that kept the three planes in place and had assumed this process would only take a few decades. 

In the main campaign, the Lucitan cult has been broken. [[Sar'eth]] has left the volcano. What remains of the Lucitan people and what they remember of what they once were is unresolved.

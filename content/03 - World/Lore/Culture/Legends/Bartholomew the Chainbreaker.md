# Bartholomew the Chainbreaker

> *Legend says she locked the dragon away and saved the world.

---

## Overview

Bartholomew was the leader of the slave revolt that ended the Elven Empire, the most significant mortal figure in recorded history, and the ancestor of a bloodline that has not stopped trying to claim her legacy ever since. She is remembered differently by different peoples: as a liberator, as a monster-slayer, as a saint, as a symbol. Almost none of what is publicly remembered is complete.

History has frequently obscured or erased that Bartholomew was a woman. The [[The Cult of the Radiant Sun|Cult of the Radiant Sun]]'s version of her tends toward a vague, genderless heroic ideal that conveniently maps onto whoever is currently leading them.

See also: [[Sar'eth]], [[The Cult of the Radiant Sun]], [[The Hooded One]], [[The Lucitans]]

---

## The Public Legend

Bartholomew led the coalition of enslaved and oppressed peoples that dismantled the Elven Empire following its weakening after the anchoring cost the elves their immortality. She is credited with uniting species that had never cooperated  humans, dwarves, goblins, drakona survivors, Aethris, and others into a force the empire could not suppress.

At the end of the revolt, legend holds that [[Sar'eth]] the dragon whose devastation of elven cities had broken the empire's back had to be contained before the shattered world could rebuild. Bartholomew is said to have **locked Sar'eth away in the volcano** through an act of heroic will or divine favor, sacrificing her own proximity to power to ensure the world's safety.

This story is told in temples, taught to children, and commemorated in monuments across the former empire's territories. It is the founding myth of several nations.

It is not what happened.

## The Bloodline

Bartholomew had descendants before she sealed herself away. Her lineage has been politically significant ever since a name that confers legitimacy, a bloodline that various powers have claimed, married into, or manufactured connections to across eight centuries.

The current inheritor of that bloodline leads the [[The Cult of the Radiant Sun]].

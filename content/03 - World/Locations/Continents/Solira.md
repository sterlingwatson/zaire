# Solira

> *The dwarves call it Tharrok: "the stone that holds." The drakona called it Vyremor: "the pyre-land."

---

## Overview

Solira is the continent on which the main campaign takes place. See the [[Maps|World Map]] for the full layout.

The Elven Empire ruled much of Solira for millennia before its collapse at IR 3139 / FR 1. The free kingdoms that now cover the continent were built in the empire's wake, and the continent is in a period of growth roughly analogous to early medieval Europe (~700s AD).

---

## Alternate Names

**Tharrok**: The dwarven name for the continent. The dwarves were created by [[Or]] from the mud and rock of the land itself, and their name reflects that relationship. To the dwarves, the continent is not a place they live on — it is the stone that holds everything together. They have been keeping the [[Dating Systems|Deep Count]] since before any other civilization began recording time, and they have called it Tharrok for every one of those years.

**Vyremor**: The drakona name for the continent, rooted in their connection to [[Elysium]] and the fires that shaped the world. Before [[Xail]]'s Great Hunt nearly destroyed them, the drakona had their own geography, their own names for every mountain range and river. Most of those names are lost. Vyremor survives because [[Sar'eth]] remembers it.

## Major Nations and Regions

### Western Solira
- **[[The Lambent Reach]]** — A large northwestern territory, encompassing or bordering Devari, Corris, Xenith, The Suncrest Kingdom, Aarosova, Verdona, Wo Ta Ki
- **[[Akavaria]]** — Island chain off the western coast; home of the Akavarii
- **[[Kingdom of Kavittany]]** — Where much of the main campaign takes place. Capitol: [[Kempeir]]
- **[[Meyshelle Mountains]]** — Mountain range north of Kavittany

### Central Solira
- **[[Forsynth Desert]]** — Desert region in the central-north
- **[[Commonwealth of the Radiant Sun]]** — A large nation tied to the [[Cult of the Radiant Sun]]
- **[[Forest of Blackest Woe]]** — Dark forest in the central region
- **[[Sivania]]** — Central region south of [[Mycilas]]
- **[[Kingdom of Gormly]]** — Southern central kingdom
- **[[Obsidia]]** — Northern territory

### Eastern Solira
- **[[Jade Empire]]** — Vast empire covering the northeast, with its own [[Dating Systems|dynastic calendar]]
- **[[The Blasted Lands]]** — Home of the Dwarven Triple Alliance
- **[[Duchy of Lortia]]** — Eastern coastal duchy
- **[[Barony of Blanc]]** — Far eastern coast
- **[[Merchant's Republic of Veche]]** — Far southeastern trade state

# The Yellowjacket's Sting Tavern

## Basic Info
- **Region:** [[Buzzing Jungle]]
- **Type:** #tavern
- **Danger Level:** #safe
- **Population:** about 15 patrons

## Description
### Visual Description
- Open air like in Central America. Straw roof, well thatched and good at sheding water. Towards the central pillar is a circle bar where the inn keepers can watch the full bar, though it never gets out of hand. One Simia bartender [[Gorse]] and one Galapa innkeeper [[Kalor]] serve drinks and assign rooms which are each a separate thatched huts. The roof is tall and many of the pillars are full of #simia patrons in hammocks or along the rafters. On the ground floor there are #galapa #ribbet and #fairy patrons. Most everyone drinks the specialty mead. In one corner is a three eyed #giant, notably not drinking the mead. Her name is [[Borth]].

### Atmosphere
- Jovial, Warm and relaxed, Peacful, Nice breeze blows in. Well landscaped with fragrant flowers all around.

### Notable Features
- 8 hut rooms surround the inn.
- Adorned with many color full fabric shades to block the sun in the day.

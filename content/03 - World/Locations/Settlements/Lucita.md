## Description
### Visual Description
- The Lavra of Lucita was a subterranean settlement which was founded in the magma caverns of [[Mt. Horoch]] in the [[Meyshelle Mountians]].
- The living areas were cave hovels carved out of basalt juts and spires that rose out of the magma flow. They look sort of like the following.
- ![[Pasted image 20250828132414.png]]
- The magma is like a lake but in many part is cooled and black on top with other spots of open hot magma.
- On the roof of the cavern which is hundreds of feet above the magma flow there are specialized lichens that are fed by the gases from the magma and grow lush like a grassland which is cultivated by the Lucitians. They are a variety of colors from blue, teal, purple, and pink.
- On these pasturelands the Lucitians raise [Bopal], which are giant aphid like creatures the size of goats that can be slaughtered for meat or milked. 

### Atmosphere
- It is hot here, regularly 110 degrees at the level the Lucitians live.
- The is a dull orange glow at the lower levels of the cavern from the magma and on the roof you can see the Lucitians and roaming bopal, who have some bioluminance like lightning bugs, in the fields like large stars.
- On a social level this is a devout community, strict religious doctrine is observed, and outsiders are generally unwelcome if they could reach here.

### Notable Features
- A large alter to [[Sar'eth]] a dragon believed to have been sealed here by folk legend [[99 - Archive/Bartholomew]]
- The Lucitians have a complicated relationship with [[Sar'eth]]. It is a worship of fear as well as reverence. They have been tasked by their ancestors with keeping the dragon asleep and locked away. For centuries this was thought to have been done with sacrifices and offerings. Sometimes of livestock, but recently of people as the [[Blessed One of Lucita]] became increasing concerned with the increase of magma and heat.
- Some trade as been known to take place with the dwarves of [[Nishunos]] along the subterranean [Orange Highway]

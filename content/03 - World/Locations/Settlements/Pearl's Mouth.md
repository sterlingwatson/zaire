# Pearl's Mouth

## Basic Info
- **Region:**  [[Kingdom of Kavittany]]
- **Population:** ~580

## Description
### Visual Description
- A village built right at the mouth of the mighty [Pearl River]. As the river flows down it fans out leading to a wide marshy delta where many folks, including [[Klix]] call home. 
- The buildings are built towards the edges of the banks in marsh alcoves. This leaves the village in two parts a right bank and left bank.
- Along the banks some of the buildings are built up on levees which then have bridges reaching out to connect houses that are built above the water.
- ![[Pasted image 20250827142035.png]]
- ![[Pasted image 20250827142042.png]]
- These buildings are built together with the trees in mind which help to slow the flow of the river. To that end there are sycomores and the like which stand in the water casting shade on the houses.
- There are also unmistakable giant mushrooms which stand over 100 ft tall.
- This is caused by run off from upstream underground caverns full of mushroom forests.
- ![[Pasted image 20250827142502.png]]
- ![[Pasted image 20250827142538.png]]
- As seen above and bellow are massive beetle like creatures called striders which have long powerful leg with large pads at the bottom used for traveling up and down the Pearl as it changes elevation.
- ![[Pasted image 20250827142750.png]]
- The residents of Pearl's Month make a living as brokers of trade between the villages up river and into its underground section and the Capitol [[Kempeir]].

### Atmosphere
- The climate is Oceanic meaning that it does often get very warm or cold, though it is fairly wet with many days of dense fog blowing off the cold ocean. There is almost no snow in the winter being right on the coast
- The water is brackish and not particularly warm. 
- The people of PM are known to hold hospitality in great esteem. They take in all strangers and feed them all manner of local delicacy from fish to [bellowhammer grubs].
- 

### Notable Features
- Most of the residents of PM are #galapa #fungril #ribbet and a few enterprising #giant. At the harbor on the right bank you can expect to see sailors from the rest of the multicultural [[Kingdom of Kavittany]], though they aren't usually ones to venture to the left bank where more of the locals hang out.
- On the left bank is the [Healer's Hut] which serves as the civic nervous system of the village. Despite its name it also contains the school and nursery as well as a few shrines to the gods and to local ancestor worship.
- [Pearl's Mouth] is known to be a place for those who wish to leave the regimented lives behind in the cities. Or a place for run aways as few outsiders tend to travel to the village, especially to the left bank. Due to the strong emphasis on hospitality these folks often become beloved member of the society as they find a new found family.

### Atmosphere
- The climate is Oceanic meaning that it does often get very warm or cold, though it is fairly wet with many days of dense fog blowing off the cold ocean. There are maybe 6 days of snow in the winter.
- The city is multicultural with many ancestries including #faun #human #firbolg #galapa #goblin #halfling #ribbet and #orc. #katari and #simia often find the rain and fog very unpleasant 
- The people of [[Kempeir]] are often quite friendly and are known to be lovers of culture. In particular there is a strong tradition of song and dance in the local pubs and squares. 
- Kempry are also known for their famous weaving and patterned clothes.
- ![[Pasted image 20250827132038.png]]
- There is a famous winter festival held in [[Kempeir]] in which the regions celebrates its cider, fishing, and culture. It lasts for 7 days culminating on the Solstice which is a holy day in the [[Zaire Pantheon]] in which [[Sehanine]] is celebrated

### Notable Features
- It is the Capitol of [[Kingdom of Kavittany]]
- Is one of the centers of the Pantheon worship, there is a large community of nuns and monks who live in the city [[Daria]] having been a member.

# Jade Empire

## Overview

A vast empire covering the north-eastern portion of [[Solira]]. The Jade Empire keeps its own [[Dating Systems|Dynastic Calendar (JE)]], counting from the first dynasty established after the Infernis invasion from the north (JE 1 = IR 3272). The [[Memory Stone]] and other artifacts of Infernis artificers originate here. Its vassals include territories to the east.

# Duchy of Vilois

## Basic Info
- **Region:**  [[Kingdom of Kavittany]]
- **Population:** 

## Description
### Visual Description
- Vilois is the eastern most region of Kavittany. 
- Noted for extensive grain fields, cute villages and vineyards/wineries. 

### Atmosphere
- Typically drier and warmer as Helliog Bay tempers the coast than the western coast of Kavittany. 

### Notable Features
- Along the coastal reaches is prime wine country and it is where the capitol [[Lovelock Estate]] stands over looking cliffs and vineyards as far as you can see. 
- There a many villages here but not a major city, the land is typically too suited for farming.
- In the interior along the road ways we find endless fields of wheat, barley and rye. Windmills dot the countryside. 
- On the east Vilois in border by the [[Kilanji Plains]] and to the northeast [[Sivania]]. Due to this there is a signifagnat population of half-eleven peoples and many #katari from the tribes found there.
- The village found at the major crossroad [[Baud]] has a large trading market where furs from the plains are traded with magic salts from the west and elven trade goods and foragables are shared.

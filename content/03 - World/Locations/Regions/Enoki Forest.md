**Arctic Oceans**

**Description:** Think overgrown mushroom forest running alongside the river. There are giant biolumenecnet mushroom trees of blueish white, orange, purples, and pinks
**Tone & Feel:** _Treacherous and forbidding. Dark and claustrophobic. Mushroom caps clog the canopy as there is not competition for light. 

**Tier:** 2
**Type:** Traversal  
**Difficulty:** 16
**Potential Adversaries:** [[Mok Harrier]] [[Mok Defender]] [[Mok Raid Leader]]

**Features:**

**Thick Groupings of Trees – Passive**  
Traveling through the forest takes a lot of skill as it is almost alien. Taveling uses a Pregress Counter (8) It ticks down according to the follwign criteria when the PCs make an action roll to navigate.
Crit: Ticks down by 3
Success with home: Down by 2
Success with Fear Down by 1
Fail with hope: No advancement
Failure with Fear: up by 1

**Speaking Spore – Passive**  
The forest itself speaks, the player are beset by words from the Mok collective. All Instict rolls that require the player to hear or think will be rolled with disadvantage.

**Ambush – Action** 
Start a 6 countdown. Each action the players take reduce it by one. When it resolves run the [[Mok Ambush]] page pg243

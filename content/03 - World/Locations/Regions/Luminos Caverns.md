## Description
### Visual Description
- Massive Caverns with ceiling which are hundreds of feet tall. They follow what turns out to be a large down hill slope. The underground portion of the Pearl River begins its head waters here. The Caverns toography largely follows this river and it's tributaries down. Toward the head of the river is [[Nishunos]] and down river is the [[Enoki Forest]]] a dense giant mushroom forest which features many bioluminescent mushroom trees. In the forest are many small #fungril villages, of particular note is [[Mok Village]] which is a village which is home to the Mok tribe, who are leading an active resistance agains the Dwarves.

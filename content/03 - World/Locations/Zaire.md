# Zaire

## Overview

Zaire is the world the name for the planet and the setting of the campaign. The primary continent of our first campaign is [[Solira]], where both the main campaign and the Legendary Heroes bonus campaign take place.

The world operates on a [[Zaire Calendar|13-month divine calendar]] of 364 days, with at least [[Dating Systems|four distinct dating systems]] in use across its civilizations.

The [[The Three Realms|Three Realms]]:  the [[Fey Wild]], the [[The Hushed Lands|Hushed Lands]], and [[Elysium]] orbit Zaire, and their proximity shapes the magical aptitude of its peoples.
